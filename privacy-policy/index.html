Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Utiliby (utiliby.com) handles when you use our website and tools.

Information you enter into our tools

Our tools run in your browser. The values you enter into a calculator, converter or generator are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers or stored by us.

Technical information

Our website is delivered through Cloudflare. Like most websites, Cloudflare and our hosting setup process standard technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for details on how it handles that data.

Accounts and cookies

We do not offer accounts. We do not currently set our own cookies. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for security purposes.

Embedded videos

Some tool pages include an optional video walkthrough hosted on YouTube. The video does not load until you click play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Once you click play, YouTube may set cookies and collect data according to Google's own privacy policy. If you do not click play, no video content or related cookies are loaded.

Analytics and advertising

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits and understand which tools are popular. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless, does not fingerprint individual users and does not track you across other websites. No data is sold or shared with advertisers. We do not run advertising on this site.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you and to improve our tools. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@utiliby.com.
