What this free typing speed test does

This typing speed test shows how fast and how accurately you type on your own keyboard. Pick a test length from 30 seconds to 10 minutes and a text type, click the box and start typing. The timer starts with your first key, and a live bar shows your time left, your words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy. When the time is up you get your net speed, gross speed, accuracy, word and character counts, a chart of your speed over the test and a table by interval.

You can copy the results, download them as a CSV file or print a summary. The whole test runs in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

How to take the test

  1. Choose a length and a text type. One minute with common words is a good first test.
  2. Sit comfortably with your wrists straight and the keyboard in front of you.
  3. Click the typing box and start. Type the text exactly as shown, including capital letters and punctuation.
  4. Fix mistakes if you notice them, but do not stop to hunt for old ones. Steady typing usually scores higher than stopping and starting.
  5. Read your result when the time ends. Press Reset or the Esc key for new text and try again.

How WPM and accuracy are calculated

In typing tests a "word" is not a real word. It is a fixed unit of five characters, spaces and punctuation included. This makes results fair whether you type short words or long ones. This test shows two speeds and an accuracy figure:

MeasureFormulaWhat it tells you
Gross WPMall characters typed / 5 / minutesRaw speed, mistakes included
Net WPMcorrect characters / 5 / minutesYour useful speed, the main result
Accuracy(keystrokes - mistyped keystrokes) / keystrokesHow clean your typing was

For example, if you type 300 characters in one minute and 285 of them match the text, your gross speed is 60 WPM and your net speed is 57 WPM. Accuracy here counts every wrong keystroke, even one you later fixed with Backspace, so it can read lower than in tests that only look at the final text. Other tests may also treat a word as an actual space-separated word or round differently, so compare scores only within one tool.

What is a good typing speed

A large study by researchers at Aalto University and the University of Cambridge recorded 136 million keystrokes from 168,000 volunteers. The average typing speed was about 51.6 WPM with 1.17% uncorrected errors, and the fastest 10% typed faster than roughly 78 WPM. Wikipedia lists 43 to 80 WPM as typical for professional typists, and mobile phone typing averaged 36.2 WPM in a 2019 study. These levels are a rough guide, not a rule:

Net WPMLevelWhat it usually means
Under 25BeginnerHunt-and-peck or just starting touch typing
25 to 39DevelopingComfortable with most keys but still looking down
40 to 54AverageTypical for everyday computer users
55 to 74GoodFine for most office and study work
75 to 99FastNear the top 10% in the large study
100 and aboveVery fastExperienced typists with long practice

A good target for most jobs and for study is 40 to 60 WPM with accuracy above 95%. A faster speed with many mistakes is worse than a slower, clean speed because fixing errors costs time.

Which test length to choose

30 seconds is a quick burst and varies a lot from run to run. 1 minute is the most common length and a good daily check, and you can take it on our 1 minute typing test. 2 and 3 minutes smooth out luck and show how your speed holds. 5 minutes tests stamina and focus and is available as the 5 minute typing test with a per-minute breakdown. 10 minutes shows real endurance, including how your accuracy changes when you tire. Longer tests usually give lower WPM than short bursts, so compare like with like.

Text types and what they train

  • Common words are the best choice for measuring raw speed because they are short, lower case and free of symbols.
  • Paragraphs use ordinary prose with capitals, commas and full stops, which is close to real writing.
  • Sentences with punctuation add commas, question marks, exclamation marks and apostrophes, so you practise the Shift key and the right-hand symbols.
  • Words and numbers mix digits, decimals and percent signs into the text. They are slower for most people and useful for data entry practice.

How to improve your typing speed

  • Accuracy first. Slow down until you make very few mistakes, then raise your pace a little. Speed follows accuracy.
  • Stop looking at the keys. Looking down breaks your rhythm. Keep your eyes on the text.
  • Read ahead. Look at the next word while you type the current one. Fast typists work a few characters ahead of their fingers.
  • Keep an even rhythm. Smooth, steady keystrokes beat bursts followed by pauses.
  • Practise a little every day. Ten focused minutes daily works better than one long session a week.
  • Work on weak keys. If the same letters or pairs keep causing errors, repeat words that use them.
  • Let your fingers overlap. The large study found that fast typists often press the next key before releasing the last one.

Touch typing, posture and comfort

Touch typing means typing without looking at the keys. Each finger has its own keys, and your fingers rest on the home row, the middle row of letters, ready to reach up or down. It takes a few weeks of practice, but it is the most reliable way to reach and keep a high speed. Good posture helps too: keep your wrists straight and relaxed, your elbows near a right angle, your shoulders loose and your screen at a comfortable height, as workplace safety guidance such as OSHA's computer workstation advice suggests. Take short breaks, because tired hands make more mistakes and long unbroken typing can strain the wrists.

Frequently asked questions

How do I test my typing speed?

Choose a test length and text type, click the typing box and start typing the text shown. The timer starts with your first key. When time is up you see your net WPM and accuracy.

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute. In typing tests one word is five characters, including spaces and punctuation, so the score does not depend on whether the words are long or short.

What is the difference between gross and net WPM?

Gross WPM counts every character you typed. Net WPM counts only the correct characters, so mistakes lower it. Net WPM is the more useful number and is the main result in this test.

How is accuracy calculated in this test?

Accuracy is the share of keystrokes that were correct. Every wrong key press counts as a mistake, even if you corrected it with Backspace afterwards.

What is a good typing speed?

For most people 40 to 60 WPM with accuracy above 95% is good. A large study found an average of about 51.6 WPM, and the fastest 10% were above roughly 78 WPM.

What is the average typing speed?

A study of 168,000 volunteers found an average of about 51.6 WPM with 1.17% uncorrected errors. Average speeds are lower on phones, with one 2019 study reporting 36.2 WPM.

How can I type faster?

Build accuracy first, stop looking at the keys, read ahead, keep a steady rhythm and practise a little every day. Learning touch typing is the most reliable long-term way to improve.

How long should the test be?

One minute is the standard choice for a quick check. Use 3 to 5 minutes to see how your speed and accuracy hold up, and 10 minutes to test endurance.

Why is my score lower on longer tests?

Fatigue, small pauses and loss of focus add up over time, so average speed over several minutes is usually lower than a short burst. Compare scores of the same length.

Should I fix my mistakes or keep going?

Fix a mistake if you notice it straight away, but do not go back to correct old ones. Stopping often costs more time than it saves. Both uncorrected and corrected mistakes affect your result.

Does this test count Backspace?

Backspace removes a character and lets you fix it, so the correct characters at the end count. The wrong key you pressed first still counts as a mistyped keystroke in your accuracy.

Why does the timer start only when I type?

So you can read the text and settle your hands without losing time. The clock starts on your first key and runs for the length you chose.

Can I paste text into the box?

No. Pasting, cutting and dropping text are turned off so the result reflects real typing.

Which text type should I pick?

Use common words to measure raw speed, paragraphs for real writing, sentences with punctuation to practise Shift and symbols, and words and numbers for data entry practice.

Is this typing test accurate?

It is accurate enough for practice and for tracking progress. It uses your browser clock, and results vary with keyboard, text and mood, so take two or three tests and look at the typical score.

Is this an official typing certificate or exam?

No. It is a free practice tool. Courses, exams and employers use their own tests and rules, so check what they require.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It runs on a phone or tablet with the on-screen keyboard, but speeds are normally lower on a touch screen. Compare phone scores with phone scores and keyboard scores with keyboard scores.

Why is my accuracy lower than in another typing test?

Tests count accuracy differently. This one counts every wrong keystroke, including ones you fixed, while others only compare the final text, which gives a higher percentage.

Do you save or upload what I type?

No. The test runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored, and the results disappear when you press Reset or close the page.

How many words is 60 WPM?

At 60 WPM you type 60 standard words of five characters each, or 300 characters, every minute. That is one standard word per second.
