What is a free zodiac sign calculator online?

A free zodiac sign calculator online is a tool that tells you which of the twelve Western zodiac signs the Sun was in on your date of birth. That sign is your sun sign, also called your star sign. The zodiac is a band of sky around the ecliptic, the Sun's apparent yearly path, divided into twelve equal slices of 30 degrees. As the Sun moves along the ecliptic it spends about a month in each slice, and the slice it was in when you were born is your sign.

Most calculators simply look your birthday up in a table of fixed dates. This one goes further. It calculates the Sun's position for your date, so it can tell you when a birthday falls on a changeover day, how close you were to a boundary, which decan you were born in, and what the Sun's exact degree was. It then adds the sign's element, modality, polarity, ruling planet, symbol and house, your Chinese zodiac animal, a compatibility checker, a table of sign dates for any year and a group list for families and friends.

It is built for anyone curious about their star sign, writers and game designers who need accurate details, and people comparing signs in a family or team. It runs in your browser, needs no sign-up, and the details you type are not sent to Utiliby. It is for entertainment and cultural interest. Astrology is not a science.

How the sun sign is calculated

The Western tropical zodiac is defined by the Sun's ecliptic longitude. Aries begins when the Sun reaches 0 degrees, at the March equinox, Taurus at 30 degrees, Gemini at 60 degrees, and so on around the circle. This calculator finds the Sun's apparent ecliptic longitude at your birth moment using a standard low-precision astronomical series with corrections for the Sun's orbit, nutation and aberration. It is accurate to about 0.01 degree. Because the Sun moves a little under one degree a day, that corresponds to roughly 15 minutes of time.

The sign is the longitude divided by 30 and rounded down. If you give no birth time, the tool checks the Sun's position at the start and end of your calendar day, in the UTC offset you chose. If the sign is the same, you get one answer. If not, you were born on a changeover day, and you get both signs and the moment of the change.

Why a fixed table can be wrong

The usual date ranges, such as Leo from July 23 to August 22, are rounded to whole days. The real boundary moves because a tropical year is not a whole number of days, and leap years pull it back. A person born on August 22 or 23 can therefore be Leo in one year and Virgo in another. Time zones add another layer: the Sun changes sign at one instant worldwide, but the calendar date differs between countries.

Limits of the calculation

  • The Sun position is accurate to roughly 15 minutes. For a birth within about a quarter of an hour of a sign change, verify the result in an ephemeris or an astrology chart program.
  • Time zones for historical births, including daylight saving rules and local mean time before standard time, can be complicated. Use the offset that applied at the place and time of birth.
  • Dates before 1900 or after 2099 are not supported.
  • The calculator finds the sun sign only. Moon sign, rising sign and houses need your birth place and a full chart.

The four zodiac elements

Each sign belongs to one of four elements, which describe how it is said to express energy.

  • Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) — passionate, energetic, action-oriented and spontaneous.
  • Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) — practical, grounded, reliable and focused on tangible results.
  • Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) — intellectual, communicative, social and idea-driven.
  • Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) — emotional, intuitive, empathetic and deeply sensitive.

In traditional compatibility, fire and air are considered friendly, as are earth and water. Signs of the same element are also said to understand each other easily.

The three modalities

Modality, also called quality or quadruplicity, describes how a sign moves through time and change. It divides the twelve signs into three groups of four.

  • Cardinal (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) — initiators. These signs begin the four seasons.
  • Fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) — stabilisers. They sit in the middle of a season and sustain what has been started.
  • Mutable (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) — adapters. They close a season and prepare for change.

Polarity and ruling planets

Polarity splits the signs into alternating Yang (active, outward: fire and air) and Yin (receptive, inward: earth and water) signs. Starting from Aries, the signs alternate Yang, Yin, Yang, Yin around the wheel.

Each sign is also linked to a ruling planet. Traditional astrology used the seven visible bodies: the Sun, the Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn. After Uranus (1781), Neptune (1846) and Pluto (1930) were discovered, modern astrology gave them to Aquarius, Pisces and Scorpio. Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006, but astrologers still use it. This calculator shows both rulers where they differ: Scorpio (Pluto, traditionally Mars), Aquarius (Uranus, traditionally Saturn) and Pisces (Neptune, traditionally Jupiter).

Decans explained

A decan is a ten-degree slice of a sign, so every sign has three. Because the Sun covers about one degree a day, decans are roughly ten-day periods. The scheme used here, common in modern astrology, assigns the first decan to the sign itself, the second to the next sign of the same element and the third to the one after. A Leo born in the second decan, for example, carries a Sagittarius sub-influence. Older Chaldean schemes assign decan rulers in a repeating sequence of the seven classical planets, so you may see different decan rulers on other sites.

Cusp births explained

A cusp birth is when your birthday falls on or near the boundary between two signs. There is no official definition. This calculator calls a birth a cusp when the Sun entered your sign, or leaves it, within about 48 hours of your birth moment, and tells you the exact gap. Astrologers say cusp people can show traits of both neighbouring signs. In technical terms, though, your sun sign is the one the Sun was in at birth, whichever side of the line you fall on.

If you were born on the day of the sign change and do not know your birth time, you are not strictly a cusp case. You are an undetermined case, and the calculator shows both possibilities.

Tropical, sidereal and the 13th sign

Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, which starts Aries at the March equinox and therefore stays tied to the seasons. Vedic (Jyotish) astrology uses the sidereal zodiac, which is measured against the stars. Because the equinox slowly drifts against the stars, a movement called precession of about one degree every 72 years, the two zodiacs are now roughly 24 degrees apart. As a result, many people have one sign in Western astrology and the previous sign in Vedic astrology. This calculator uses the tropical zodiac only.

You may also hear that there are thirteen signs. The Sun does pass through the constellation Ophiuchus for a couple of weeks between late November and mid December, using modern constellation boundaries. But the zodiac signs are twelve equal 30 degree divisions of the ecliptic tied to the seasons, not constellation outlines, and the constellations themselves have unequal sizes. So Western astrology does not use Ophiuchus.

Sun, moon and rising signs

  • Sun sign — where the Sun was at birth. It is said to describe your core identity. This is what people usually mean by "zodiac sign".
  • Moon sign — where the Moon was. It is said to describe your emotional world.
  • Rising sign (ascendant) — the sign rising on the eastern horizon at your exact birth time and place. It is said to describe how you appear to others.

The Moon changes sign about every two and a half days and the rising sign roughly every two hours, so both need an exact birth time and place. This tool calculates the sun sign. It is the natural first step, and it is the one that needs only a date.

Chinese zodiac by birth year

The Chinese zodiac runs on a twelve-year cycle, with each year tied to an animal. It follows the lunisolar calendar, so the animal year begins on Chinese New Year, which falls between about January 21 and February 20, and not on January 1. If you were born in January or early February, check the date: you may belong to the previous year's animal. The calculator uses a table of Chinese New Year dates from 1900 to 2099 and tells you when your birthday falls before the new year.

The twelve animals in order are Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. By tradition the order comes from a folk story about a race. Each animal year is also assigned a Yin or Yang polarity (even-numbered years in the ten-year stem cycle are Yang) and one of five elements: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal or Water, each lasting two years in a row. Together the twelve animals and five elements make a sixty-year cycle, which is why a Metal Rat comes back only every sixty years. Some Chinese astrology traditions, such as BaZi, start the year at Lichun around February 4 instead of Chinese New Year, so you may see a different animal in those systems.

The twelve sign profiles

These short descriptions summarise the traditional associations of each sign. They are generalisations, and no sign describes everyone born under it.

♈ Aries: Mar 21 to Apr 19

Aries opens the zodiac and the spring season, so it is associated with beginnings, drive and directness. Aries people are described as quick to act, quick to forgive and happiest when there is a challenge in front of them.

Strengths: takes the initiative; brave under pressure; energetic and optimistic. Challenges: impatience; impulsiveness; can be competitive to a fault. Symbol: The Ram. Natural house: 1. Ruling day (traditional): Tuesday.

♉ Taurus: Apr 20 to May 20

Taurus is the fixed earth sign of mid-spring. Tradition links it to patience, the senses and a love of stability, from good food to a secure home. Taurus is often described as slow to start but very hard to stop.

Strengths: steady and dependable; appreciates quality and comfort; persistent. Challenges: resistance to change; stubbornness; possessiveness. Symbol: The Bull. Natural house: 2. Ruling day (traditional): Friday.

♊ Gemini: May 21 to Jun 20

Gemini is the mutable air sign that closes spring. It is tied to communication, curiosity and variety. Geminis are described as talkative, sociable and always interested in the next question or idea.

Strengths: quick learner; excellent communicator; flexible. Challenges: restlessness; scattered focus; indecision. Symbol: The Twins. Natural house: 3. Ruling day (traditional): Wednesday.

♋ Cancer: Jun 21 to Jul 22

Cancer begins the northern summer and is the cardinal water sign. It is the only sign ruled by the Moon, which is why it is linked with home, memory and emotional care. Cancers are often described as protective and deeply loyal.

Strengths: caring and protective; emotionally perceptive; loyal to family and friends. Challenges: moodiness; holding on to the past; over-sensitivity. Symbol: The Crab. Natural house: 4. Ruling day (traditional): Monday.

♌ Leo: Jul 23 to Aug 22

Leo is the fixed fire sign of high summer and the only sign ruled by the Sun itself. It stands for self-expression, creativity and heart. Leos are described as warm, proud and happiest when they can lead or perform.

Strengths: warm and generous; natural leader; creative and expressive. Challenges: pride; need for attention; stubborn streak. Symbol: The Lion. Natural house: 5. Ruling day (traditional): Sunday.

♍ Virgo: Aug 23 to Sep 22

Virgo is the mutable earth sign at the end of summer, associated with service, craft and analysis. Virgos are described as careful, modest and quietly effective, with a habit of noticing what needs improving.

Strengths: detail-oriented; helpful and reliable; practical problem solver. Challenges: over-criticism; worry; perfectionism. Symbol: The Maiden. Natural house: 6. Ruling day (traditional): Wednesday.

♎ Libra: Sep 23 to Oct 22

Libra starts autumn at the equinox and is the cardinal air sign. It is linked with balance, relationships and aesthetics. Libras are described as diplomatic and charming, and as people who dislike conflict and unfairness.

Strengths: fair-minded; gracious and sociable; good at seeing both sides. Challenges: indecision; avoiding conflict; people-pleasing. Symbol: The Scales. Natural house: 7. Ruling day (traditional): Friday.

♏ Scorpio: Oct 23 to Nov 21

Scorpio is the fixed water sign of deep autumn. It is associated with intensity, transformation and privacy. Scorpios are described as determined and perceptive, with strong feelings that they do not always show.

Strengths: determined and focused; perceptive; fiercely loyal. Challenges: secrecy; jealousy; all-or-nothing intensity. Symbol: The Scorpion. Natural house: 8. Ruling day (traditional): Tuesday.

♐ Sagittarius: Nov 22 to Dec 21

Sagittarius is the mutable fire sign that closes autumn. It stands for exploration, philosophy and freedom. Sagittarians are described as optimistic, frank and always looking toward the next horizon.

Strengths: optimistic and enthusiastic; honest and open; loves learning and travel. Challenges: bluntness; restlessness; over-promising. Symbol: The Archer. Natural house: 9. Ruling day (traditional): Thursday.

♑ Capricorn: Dec 22 to Jan 19

Capricorn begins at the winter solstice and is the cardinal earth sign. It is linked with structure, ambition and responsibility. Capricorns are described as patient planners who build things step by step.

Strengths: disciplined and persistent; responsible; long-term planner. Challenges: workaholism; pessimism; reserve. Symbol: The Sea-Goat. Natural house: 10. Ruling day (traditional): Saturday.

♒ Aquarius: Jan 20 to Feb 18

Aquarius is the fixed air sign of mid-winter. It is associated with ideas, community and reform. Aquarians are described as independent and inventive, and as people who like to think differently from the crowd.

Strengths: original thinker; community-minded; independent. Challenges: emotional distance; contrariness; stubborn views. Symbol: The Water-Bearer. Natural house: 11. Ruling day (traditional): Saturday.

♓ Pisces: Feb 19 to Mar 20

Pisces is the mutable water sign that closes the zodiac and the winter season. It is linked with imagination, empathy and endings that flow into new beginnings. Pisceans are described as gentle, intuitive and creative.

Strengths: compassionate; imaginative and artistic; adaptable. Challenges: escapism; over-giving; vagueness. Symbol: The Fishes. Natural house: 12. Ruling day (traditional): Thursday.

The Sun's position is calculated with a standard low-precision astronomical formula that is good to about 0.01 degree, which is roughly 15 minutes of time. That is enough to find the right sign for everyone except births within about 15 minutes of a sign change. For those, check an ephemeris or your birth chart.

Why does the tool ask for a UTC offset?

The Sun changes sign at one instant worldwide, but the calendar date depends on your time zone. A person born at 11 pm in New York and a person born at 4 am in London the next day can share the same moment. Choose the offset that applied at your birthplace on your birth date, including daylight saving time.

What should I do if I do not know my birth time?

Leave the time empty. For most dates the sign is the same all day. On the one or two changeover days each month, the calculator shows both possible signs and the time the Sun changed sign, so you can compare it with what you know about your birth.

How do the compatibility results work?

The Compatibility tab compares two Sun signs by their angle around the zodiac and by element, modality and polarity. Signs that are 120 degrees apart (trine) share an element and are called easy. Signs 90 degrees apart (square) share a modality and are called dynamic. Results are traditional interpretations, not predictions.

Can I check the sign of several people at once?

Yes. Use the Group list tab to add names and dates of birth. You get a table with sun sign, element, modality and Chinese animal for each person, a chart of how the elements and modalities are spread, and CSV, copy and print options.

What are the exact dates the Sun enters each sign this year?

Open the Sign dates by year tab and enter any year from 1900 to 2099. You get the calculated moment the Sun enters and leaves each sign in the UTC offset you choose, including the equinox and solstice markers.

Why do astrology websites sometimes show different dates?

Sources round the sign changes to whole days and use different years or time zones. The Sun's actual change moment moves between years. This calculator uses the astronomical position for your exact date and offset, so it can differ from a fixed table on boundary days.
