  1. Pick Metric or Imperial units.

  4. Adjust protein if you train. Results appear instantly below the form.

How the macros are calculated

Want the full explanation behind these numbers? Read our complete guide to calculating keto macros.

The calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for basal metabolic rate (BMR), multiplies by an activity factor for maintenance, adjusts by a goal multiplier, then splits the result into protein, fat and carbs.

  • Men: BMR = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5
  • Women: BMR = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161
  • Protein = g/kg target × body weight
  • Carbs = fixed by diet preset
  • Fat = remaining calories ÷ 9

Frequently asked questions

How many carbs should I eat on keto?

Most people aim for 20 to 50 grams of net carbs per day.

Should I use net carbs or total carbs?

Keto plans usually count net carbs (total carbs minus fiber).

Is a macro calculator accurate?

It is an estimate. Track for 2 to 3 weeks, then adjust.

Is anything I enter stored?

No. All calculations run in your browser.
