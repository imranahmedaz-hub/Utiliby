Productivity & Organization Tools

Free online tools to focus, plan projects, manage tasks, build habits, schedule your week and make better decisions. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up, no tracking.

Pomodoro TimerFocus in timed sessions with breaks, sounds, a task log and stats. Online To-Do ListAdd tasks with priority, due date, list and repeat. Saved in your browser. Gantt Chart MakerBuild a Gantt chart with dependencies, milestones and progress. Export PNG. Kanban BoardDrag cards across custom columns with labels, due dates and WIP limits. Habit TrackerTrack habits in a monthly grid with streaks, goals and progress charts. Weekly Schedule MakerMake a colour-coded weekly timetable with clash checks. Download PNG. Eisenhower Matrix MakerSort tasks into Do, Schedule, Delegate and Eliminate. Download PNG. Meeting Agenda GeneratorCreate a timed meeting agenda from 10 templates. Copy, ICS or print. Decision Matrix CalculatorCompare options against weighted criteria with a ranked result and chart. Meeting Scheduler PollPoll attendees to find the best meeting time.
