Marketing & Advertising Tools

Free online tools for marketers, founders and small businesses: make QR codes and email signatures, name a company, test and measure campaigns, write better headlines and ads, and build buyer personas. Everything runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no tracking.

QR Code GeneratorMake a free QR code for links, Wi-Fi, contacts, WhatsApp and more with logo, colours and frame. Download PNG or SVG. Email Signature GeneratorBuild a professional HTML email signature with photo, social links and a button, preview it in light and dark mode and copy it to Gmail or Outlook. Business Name GeneratorTurn keywords into brandable business names with scores, matching domain and social handle formats and a shortlist you can export. A/B Test CalculatorCheck A/B test significance, p-value, lift and confidence interval, and plan sample size and test length. Marketing ROI & ROAS CalculatorCalculate ROI, ROAS and break-even ROAS, set a budget goal, and compare customer acquisition cost with lifetime value. Headline & Subject Line AnalyzerScore headlines, email subject lines and ad headlines out of 100, with previews for Google, inboxes and social. Google Ads Character CounterCount Google Ads headline, description and path limits live and preview desktop and mobile responsive search ads. CTR, CPM, CPC & CPA CalculatorGet CTR, CPC, CPM, CPA, conversion rate and ROAS from your totals, forecast a budget and convert CPM to CPC. Buyer Persona GeneratorBuild a printable buyer persona card with goals, pain points, channels and buyer journey, from a template or blank. Elevator Pitch GeneratorWrite a clear 30-second elevator pitch for investors, customers or networking events.
