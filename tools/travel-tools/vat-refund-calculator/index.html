What this free online tourist VAT refund calculator does

Shopping abroad often comes with a promise: tax free. In practice, the refund you actually receive is smaller than the VAT rate on the sign, and a surprising number of travellers lose it altogether by missing a minimum spend, a stamp or a deadline. This free online VAT refund calculator shows you the real figure before you spend, and keeps track of the details that decide whether you get it.

Enter the country, the receipt total including VAT, and the type of refund. The tool works out the VAT inside the price, subtracts the refund company's fee, and shows your estimated net refund in the local currency and as a percentage of what you paid. It checks the minimum spend, works out the date by which your goods must leave the EU, and compares 29 countries so you can see where the refund is worth chasing. A bar that splits the price into the cost without VAT, the fee and your refund makes the numbers easy to grasp at a glance.

You can enter several receipts, see which ones qualify, download them as a spreadsheet, add the first deadline to your calendar and print or save a one-page report with only your result. Everything is calculated in your browser.

  1. Choose the country where you bought the goods. The standard VAT rate and the typical minimum spend are filled in.

How tourist VAT refunds work, step by step

VAT is a tax built into the price of almost everything you buy in the EU. When a visitor takes new goods home in their luggage, the sale is treated as an export, and the VAT can be returned. The system is run by shops that join a tax-free scheme and by refund companies that process the paperwork for a fee.

  • At the shop: ask for a tax-free form before you pay and show your passport. The shop fills in your details and the purchase.
  • Before you leave: keep the goods unused, with the receipt and the form.
  • At customs: when you leave the EU, the customs officer checks the goods and stamps the form. Do this before you check your baggage.
  • At the refund desk: hand the stamped form to the refund company's desk or drop box. Choose a card refund or cash.
  • After you get home: check that the refund arrives on your card. Some take several weeks.

If your trip crosses several EU countries, you usually have the forms stamped only once, at your last point of exit from the EU.

How the refund is calculated

A price with VAT included contains price × rate ÷ (100 + rate) of VAT. At 22% in Italy, a 500 euro receipt contains 500 × 22 ÷ 122 = 90.16 euros of VAT. This is the most you could ever get back. The refund company then keeps a fee, commonly around a quarter of the VAT, so you might receive about 66 euros, or 13 percent of the price. Cash refunds usually cost extra.

Rules that decide whether you get the refund

  • Residence. The scheme is for people who live outside the EU. Your passport and address matter.
  • Goods only. Services, hotels, meals and tickets are not refundable.
  • Unused. Goods should be new. Some customs officers check, especially for clothes and electronics.
  • Personal luggage. Items must leave in your baggage within the time limit. Business or commercial quantities are excluded.
  • Minimum per receipt. The minimum applies to one tax-free form.
  • Deadline. In the EU, by the end of the third month after the purchase month.
  • Stamp before check-in. If the item is in a checked bag, you must show it to customs first, so keep it with you until then.

Tips to get more of your VAT back

  • Shop where the minimum is low and the VAT is high for the goods you want.
  • Ask the shop to put several items on one receipt to pass the minimum.
  • Choose a card refund to avoid the cash fee.
  • Arrive early at the airport, because customs queues can be long.
  • Photograph the stamped form and keep it until the money arrives.
  • Use e-validation machines if your airport has them.
  • Do not buy more than you need just to reach a minimum.

Common mistakes that cost travellers money

  • Packing the goods in checked luggage before the customs stamp.
  • Forgetting to ask for the form at the till.
  • Missing the deadline because the trip lasted more than a few weeks.
  • Assuming the refund equals the VAT rate.
  • Mixing receipts from different shops to meet the minimum.
  • Holding the form for the wrong country when you fly out via another EU airport.

Accuracy, limits and responsible use

The calculator uses typical VAT rates, minimum spends and fee levels. It does not know your shop's own terms, the product's rate, your residence or customs practice on the day. It covers goods only, and results are estimates. Use the override boxes to match your receipt, and confirm the figures on your tax-free form before you plan a purchase around them.

Frequently asked questions

What is a tourist VAT refund?

It is a refund of the value added tax you paid on goods you buy in a country and take home in your luggage. It is open to visitors who live outside the country or the EU, and it covers goods, not services.

Who can claim a VAT refund on purchases?

Generally people whose permanent home is outside the EU, and who leave the EU within the deadline with the goods. Citizens of the EU living abroad can sometimes qualify, so ask the shop.

How much VAT can I get back?

The VAT already inside the price, which is the price × rate ÷ (100 + rate). A 20% VAT purchase of 120 has 20 of VAT. After the refund company fee you usually keep roughly 10 to 15 percent of the price.

Why is my refund smaller than the VAT rate?

Because the rate applies to the price without VAT, so the VAT in a price including VAT is a smaller share, and because the refund company keeps a handling fee, typically around a quarter of the VAT.

What is the minimum spend for a VAT refund?

It differs by country. Spain has none, France is about 100 euros, Italy about 70 euros, Germany about 50 euros and others are higher. The minimum usually applies to a single receipt from a single shop.

Can I add receipts from different shops together?

Usually not. Each shop issues its own tax-free form, and the minimum is checked on each one. Several receipts from the same shop on the same day can normally be combined.

What is the deadline for claiming a refund?

In the EU, goods generally have to leave the Union by the end of the third month after the month of purchase. Norway, Switzerland and Iceland have shorter or different periods, so confirm with the shop.

Where do I get the customs stamp?

At the airport or border where you leave the EU, before you check in any bag that holds the goods. If you are flying through another EU country, validation happens at your last exit point from the EU.

Do I have to show the goods at customs?

Often yes. Keep the items unused and easy to reach in your hand luggage, together with the receipts, the tax-free form, your passport and your boarding pass. Many airports now offer electronic validation kiosks.

What is Global Blue or Planet?

They are the main companies that run tax-free shopping. They process the refund for the retailer and take a fee from the VAT, then pay the rest to your card or in cash.

Is cash or card refund better?

A card refund is usually cheaper. Cash at the airport desk normally carries an extra fee. Card refunds can take several weeks.

Can I get VAT back on hotels, food or services?

No. Tourist refunds apply to goods that you export. VAT on hotels, restaurants, tours and transport is not refundable to visitors.

Is there a VAT refund in the UK?

No. The UK ended tax-free shopping for visitors from overseas at the start of 2021. Some EU countries still offer it, so plan your shopping accordingly.

Do reduced VAT rates affect my refund?

Yes. Some goods such as books and certain food have lower rates. Use the rate box in the tool to enter the rate printed on your receipt, and the refund adjusts.

What if my purchase falls just below the minimum?

The shop cannot issue a form, so there is no refund. The tool shows how much more to spend on the same receipt. Do not buy things just to pass the minimum unless you want them.

Is the result exact?

No, it is an estimate. Rates, minimums and fees change and every refund company sets its own terms. Check your tax-free form for the exact amount.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Nothing is saved after you close the page.
