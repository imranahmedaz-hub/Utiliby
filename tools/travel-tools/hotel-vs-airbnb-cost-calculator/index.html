What this free hotel vs Airbnb cost calculator does

The nightly rate on a booking site is rarely what you end up paying. Hotels add taxes, resort fees and parking, and rentals add cleaning fees, service fees and extra guest charges. Then there is food: a kitchen can cut the bill, while a breakfast buffet can too. This free online hotel vs Airbnb cost calculator adds everything up so you can compare like for like. Enter the figures for a hotel and a vacation rental and it shows the total, the cost per night, the cost per person and the break-even length of stay.

A stacked chart splits each option into room cost, taxes and fees, cleaning, parking and food. A second tab shows how the all-in cost per night changes with the length of stay, which is where cleaning fees matter most. A third tab shows how the answer changes as the group grows from one to eight people, since a rental can house everyone in one booking while a hotel needs more rooms. Everything runs in your browser, and no live prices are used or stored.

How to use the hotel vs Airbnb cost calculator

  1. Enter the nights, guests and your currency.
  2. Fill in the hotel: nightly rate, guests per room, taxes, resort fee, parking and tips.
  3. Fill in the rental: nightly rate, cleaning fee, service fee, taxes, discounts and extra guest fee.
  4. Enter your food costs and the share of meals you would cook.
  5. Read the total, break-even and chart.
  6. Check the other tabs for length of stay and group size.

How the totals are calculated

For the hotel the tool multiplies the room rate by the number of rooms and nights, applies any long-stay discount, adds tax, adds the resort fee for each room and night, plus parking and tips, and then adds food. For the rental it applies the weekly or monthly discount to the nightly total, adds the cleaning fee once, adds extra guest charges, then service fee and tax on that subtotal, plus parking and food.

For the default example of 7 nights for 2 guests, the hotel at $185 a night costs $1,295 for the room, $181 in tax, $385 in resort fees and parking, $21 in tips and $504 for food with breakfast included, a total of $2,386. The rental at $150 a night has a 10 percent weekly discount, so the nightly cost is $945. Cleaning of $90 and a 14 percent service fee ($145) and 12 percent tax ($124) bring the stay to $1,304, and with food and groceries of $502 the total is $1,806. The rental saves $580, or about $83 a night.

When a hotel wins and when a rental wins

Hotels tend to win on short stays, because a rental's cleaning fee is a large share of a one- or two-night booking, on solo trips, and where flexible cancellation, daily housekeeping, a front desk and loyalty rewards matter. Rentals tend to win for stays of a week or more, for families and groups who would need several hotel rooms, for trips where you want to cook and do laundry, and in destinations where hotels are expensive and fees are high. Use the length of stay and group size tabs to find the crossover for your own numbers, and always compare the final price shown at checkout, including all fees.

Tips to get the best deal

  • Compare the total price, not the nightly rate.
  • Check for resort and cleaning fees before you book.
  • Ask for discounts on weekly or monthly stays.
  • Consider the location, since transport costs add up.
  • Read cancellation terms, which can be worth the extra cost.
  • Check local rules for short-term rentals and the host's reviews.

Hidden costs and checks before you book

  • Cleaning and service fees. Check the total price shown at checkout, as some platforms add fees late in the booking.
  • Resort and destination fees. Hotels may add them to the price at the property, not on the booking site.
  • Deposits. A security deposit is not a cost if all goes well, but it can tie up money on your card.
  • Check-in logistics. Late arrivals, key handovers and self-check-in can add stress or extra fees.
  • Parking and transport. A cheaper rental far from the centre can cost more in taxis and time.
  • Cancellation terms. A flexible rate can be worth the extra cost if your plans might change.
  • Supplies. Check for towels, linen, cooking basics and laundry, which a hotel supplies without a charge.
  • Local rules and safety. Look at reviews, licences and local short-term rental regulations.

Frequently asked questions

Is a hotel or Airbnb cheaper?

It depends on the length of stay, the group size and the fees. Rentals tend to win for longer stays, groups and trips where you cook, while hotels often win for short stays, solo travellers and places with high cleaning fees.

How does the calculator compare them?

It adds the room or nightly rate, taxes, service and cleaning fees, resort fees, parking, tips and food for each option, then shows the total, the cost per night and per person, and the break-even stay.

What is the break-even?

The number of nights from which the rental becomes cheaper overall. Because a cleaning fee is charged once, longer stays spread it over more nights.

Why do cleaning fees matter?

A flat cleaning fee of $90 adds $90 to a one-night stay but only about $13 a night for a week. On short stays it can wipe out the nightly saving.

What is a resort fee?

A mandatory daily charge some hotels add on top of the room rate for facilities such as pool or Wi-Fi. It can add $25 to $50 per night, and it often does not show in the headline price.

How are taxes handled?

Enter the tax percentage for each. The tool applies the hotel tax to the room cost and the rental tax to the nightly cost plus cleaning and extra guest charges.

How does food change the answer?

A kitchen lets you cook some meals. The tool compares the cost of eating out with cooking at home and lets you choose the share of meals cooked in the rental. A hotel with breakfast included trims the food bill by about a fifth.

How many rooms do I need?

The hotel needs one room for each group of up to your guests-per-room number. A group of four with two guests per room needs two rooms.

What about extra guests in a rental?

Many rentals include a number of guests and charge a fee per extra guest per night. Enter the guests included and the fee.

Does the tool include long-stay discounts?

Yes. Enter a weekly discount for 7 nights or more and a monthly discount for 28 nights or more for the rental, and a 7+ night discount for the hotel.

What is not included?

Loyalty points, free cancellation, airport transfers, deposits, insurance and the value of location and service. Consider these alongside price.

How does group size change the result?

The Group size tab shows hotel and rental totals for 1 to 8 guests, so you can see where a rental becomes more economical per person.

Can I use it for other rentals?

Yes. It works for any vacation rental, serviced apartment or home exchange where you know the nightly rate and fees.

Are the figures live prices?

No. You enter prices from the sites you are comparing, and the tool does not connect to any platform.

Should I only consider price?

No. Safety, location, cancellation terms, local rules for short-term rentals and the quality of the host or hotel matter too.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser.

Is this hotel vs Airbnb calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use online with no sign-up.
