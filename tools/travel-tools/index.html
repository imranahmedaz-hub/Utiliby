Travel Tools

Free calculators and utilities for smarter travel. Every tool runs in your browser, so nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

Jet Lag CalculatorEstimate recovery time and get personalized tips to beat jet lag. Schengen 90/180 Day Calculator Rolling-window overstay check. Days used, days remaining, next re-entry date. Layover Time Calculator Is your connection long enough? Minimum connection times, terminal transfers, ticket warnings. Passport Validity Checker 6-month rule, 3-month rule, Schengen 10-year rule. Global coverage, visual timeline. EU261 Compensation Calculator €250, €400 or €600 for flight disruption. Distance calculator, claim letter generator. VAT Refund Calculator Real net refund after operator fees. 25+ countries, minimum spend thresholds. Flight Time CalculatorEstimate flight time, distance and local arrival time between 114 airports, with a route map and multi-stop trips. Packing List GeneratorBuild a printable packing checklist by trip type, weather and travellers, then check the weight against your baggage limit. Travel Budget CalculatorPlan trip costs by category, build a savings plan, check currency fees and compare 13 world regions. Carry-On Size CheckerCheck your bag against 32 airline cabin limits in cm and inches with weight and personal item sizes. Airport Arrival Time CalculatorWork out when to leave home and reach the airport, with a timeline and exit time after landing. Sunrise and Sunset CalculatorSunrise, sunset, day length, twilight, golden hour and a live sun path for any city or coordinates. Flight Carbon Footprint CalculatorEstimate flight CO2 by route and class, compare with car and rail, and total a year of flying. Tipping Guide CalculatorHow much to tip in 63 countries for restaurants, bars and taxis, plus a trip tipping budget. Points and Miles Value CalculatorFind cents per point, test a rewards card against cash back and see if buying points pays off. Currency Exchange Fee CalculatorSee the real cost of changing money, compare 8 methods, avoid conversion traps and total trip fees. Days in Country TrackerCount days per country over any tax year or rolling window and see days left before the 183-day limit. Hotel vs Airbnb Cost CalculatorCompare hotel and vacation rental total cost with fees, taxes and food, and find the break-even stay.
