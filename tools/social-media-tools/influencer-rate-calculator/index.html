What this free influencer rate calculator does

Few questions cause more guesswork for creators than what to charge a brand, and few cause more uncertainty for brands than what to pay. The honest answer is that there is no standard price list, because rates depend on audience, engagement, effort, rights and demand. This free online influencer rate calculator gives you a structured starting point. It begins with the size of your audience on the platform you choose, adjusts for engagement quality, then applies factors for content type, niche, usage rights, exclusivity and rush delivery.

The result is a fair rate with a low and high range, a package total for several deliverables, your creator tier, the cost per 1,000 followers and a step-by-step chart that shows how each factor changed the number. Because you can see the steps, you can explain your rate to a brand or test what happens when you change the terms. The calculation runs in your browser.

How to use the calculator

  1. Choose the platform. Rates per follower differ between networks.
  2. Enter your audience. Followers or subscribers on that platform.
  3. Add engagement. Type your engagement rate or the average engagements per post. If you leave it blank, the platform benchmark is used.
  4. Pick the content type and niche. A short video costs more than a single story, and finance costs more than general lifestyle.
  5. Set rights and exclusivity. Add paid usage or a no-competitor period if the brand wants them.
  6. Set the package. Choose how many deliverables and any package discount.
  7. Read the range. Use the middle figure as your target and the range to plan negotiation.

Why engagement changes the price

A follower count says how many people could see a post. Engagement says how many care. Brands know that an account with 100,000 followers and 0.5 percent engagement reaches fewer active people than one with 20,000 followers and 6 percent. This tool compares your engagement rate with a typical value for the platform and scales the rate up or down, within limits, so a strong community is rewarded without letting one number dominate. If you do not know your rate, use the engagement rate calculator on this site, and remember that platform benchmarks are only rough.

Usage rights, whitelisting and exclusivity

The creation fee pays for your time and your audience. Anything beyond a post on your own feed is a separate service. If a brand wants to repost your content, run it as an ad through your handle or use it for a long period, it receives value that outlasts the post, and the price should reflect it. Exclusivity stops you from working with competitors, so you give up potential income. The tool adds each as a percentage of the creation fee, and the chart shows how much each adds. Always agree these terms in writing, including the length of time and the channels.

Negotiating with brands

Start with the middle of your range and be ready to explain it. A brand with a small budget may offer less, and you can trade price for fewer deliverables, shorter usage or no exclusivity rather than just dropping the fee. Ask what the goal is, whether awareness, sales or content, and suggest a format that serves it. Keep a record of past results such as reach, clicks and sales, because proof of performance supports a higher rate. If a request is outside your usual work, charge for the extra effort instead of absorbing it.

For brands and agencies: planning a budget

If you buy influencer content, use the calculator to check whether a quote is within a reasonable range, and to plan a budget for a set of creators. Compare the cost per thousand followers and, if you know the engagement, the cost per engagement across creators. Do not choose on follower count alone. Look at audience location, content quality, past brand work and fit with your product. Use contracts that set deliverables, dates, rights and approval steps, and require clear disclosure of the partnership.

Disclosure and legal basics

In many countries, sponsored posts must be clearly labeled so the audience knows about the relationship. Use the platform's paid partnership label and plain words such as ad or sponsored near the start of the post, and not only hidden among hashtags. Both the creator and the brand can be responsible for compliance. Tax, contracts and employment status vary by country, so ask a qualified professional for advice before you sign large deals.

Common pricing mistakes

  • Underpricing for exposure. Exposure does not pay bills. Know your minimum.
  • Ignoring usage rights. Giving away paid-ad rights for free is costly.
  • Pricing on followers only. Engagement and results matter.
  • No written terms. Misunderstandings follow.
  • Forgetting taxes and fees. Plan for them in your rate.
  • Never reviewing prices. Update your rate as your audience grows.

Privacy, limits and responsible use

The calculator works in your browser and does not store or send your numbers. The rates per follower, tier multipliers and factors are general rules of thumb created for this tool, not data from any platform or agency, and the market changes. Treat the output as an estimate and a talking point, not a quote. Currency conversion uses the rate you enter. Follow the advertising and tax rules that apply in your country.

Frequently asked questions

What is an influencer rate calculator?

A tool that estimates what a creator may charge for sponsored content. This free one uses followers, engagement, platform, content type, niche, usage rights and exclusivity to give a range per deliverable and a package total.

How do I use this free influencer rate calculator online?

Choose the platform, enter followers and an engagement rate, pick the content type and niche, and select any usage rights, exclusivity or rush fee. The fair rate and range update instantly.

How much should an influencer charge per post?

There is no fixed price. A common starting rule is about 100 dollars per 10,000 followers, adjusted for engagement and content type. This tool starts from follower rates by platform and adjusts from there.

What are the influencer tiers?

Nano is under 10,000 followers, micro is 10,000 to 50,000, mid-tier is 50,000 to 500,000, macro is 500,000 to 1 million and mega is over 1 million. Smaller creators usually earn more per follower.

Why does engagement matter?

Brands pay for attention and action, not only reach. A smaller account with an engaged community can be worth more than a large one with few interactions, so the tool adjusts the rate by your engagement compared with the platform benchmark.

How do I calculate my engagement rate?

Add likes, comments, shares and saves on a post, divide by followers and multiply by 100. You can also enter the average engagements and let the tool do it. The engagement rate calculator on this site helps.

What are usage rights?

Permission for the brand to reuse your content, for example on its own channels or in paid ads. Because it increases the value to the brand, usage rights are priced on top of the creation fee.

What is whitelisting?

When a brand runs ads through your handle, so the ad appears to come from you. It is a common paid usage right and is priced separately from the post.

What is exclusivity?

An agreement not to work with competing brands for a period. It limits your income, so creators add a fee that rises with the length of the period.

Why are video integrations priced higher than static posts?

They take more time and skill to produce and often deliver longer attention. The tool multiplies the rate for short videos, integrations and dedicated videos.

Does the niche change the rate?

Yes. Brands in finance, business and technology pay more per audience member than brands in broad lifestyle niches. Niche multipliers in this tool are rough guides.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a rule-of-thumb starting point, not a price list. Real fees depend on negotiation, demand, past results and the budget of the brand. Use it to prepare, not to replace judgment.

Should I charge less if the brand gives me free product?

That depends on the value of the product and how much work is needed. Many creators accept product only for small tasks and ask for payment as well for time and usage rights.

Do I need a contract?

Yes. Agree deliverables, dates, fees, usage rights, exclusivity, revisions and payment terms in writing. Legal advice from a qualified professional is recommended for large deals.

Do I have to disclose sponsored content?

In most countries, yes. Use the platform's paid partnership tools or clear labels such as ad or sponsored, and follow local advertising rules.

Is my data uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in your browser.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use online and needs no sign-up.
