What this free Instagram line break generator does


You type a bio with neat gaps between lines, paste it into Instagram and the gaps vanish. This frustrating behavior is why people search for an Instagram line break generator. Instagram trims lines that are completely empty, especially in bios, so your layout collapses into a block. The usual fix is to put an invisible character on each empty line. This free online tool does that for you, in one click, and gives you more control than a simple spacer: choose the invisible character, collapse or add blank lines, move hashtags below a block of dots and see a count against the limit for your bio, caption, comment or another network.
The text is processed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and you can see exactly what will be copied, with the invisible characters shown as dots so that you can check them.


How to use the line break generator


  1. Paste your text. Type it with the blank lines you want.
  2. Keep the spacer option on. Empty lines are filled with an invisible character.
  3. Choose how to treat blank lines. Keep them, collapse extra ones, add one between every line or remove them.
  4. Pick a limit. The counter tells you if the bio or caption is too long.
  5. Copy. Paste into the Instagram app.
  6. Check. Open your profile or the post and make sure the spacing looks right.


Why Instagram removes blank lines


Instagram cleans text when you save it. Leading and trailing spaces are removed, and in bios any line that has no visible content may be collapsed. This keeps profiles tidy, but it also breaks deliberate layouts. A character that looks empty but is technically text, such as the Braille blank pattern, gives the line content, so the platform keeps it. Behavior has changed over time. Captions often keep blank lines if you type them in the app, while bios and text pasted from other apps are more likely to lose them, which is why a tool helps.


Which invisible character to use


CharacterNotes
Braille blank (U+2800)Most reliable. Takes space and is not trimmed.
Hangul filler (U+3164)Works in many apps. Can look slightly wider.
Em space (U+2003)A real space, but may be trimmed in some fields.
Zero-width space (U+200B)Takes no width, may be removed by some apps.
If one does not work on your device, try another. Always test in the app before you rely on it.


Hiding hashtags below dots


Many creators put hashtags after a few lines of dots or bullets so that the caption looks clean and the tags sit below the more link. This tool finds lines that contain only hashtags, removes them from the body and places them at the end after the number of dot lines you choose. You can use periods, bullets or invisible spacers for the dots. The tags still belong to the post, and Instagram treats them as hashtags. Keep to relevant tags, and do not add dozens of unrelated ones.


Layout ideas for bios and captions


  • Bio: one line for who you are, one for what you offer, one for a call to action, with a blank line between groups.
  • Short captions: one idea per line with blank lines between, which reads well on a phone.
  • Long captions: a hook first, short paragraphs, a list for steps and a question at the end.
  • Lists: use bullets or arrows at the start of each line, with no blank lines between items.


Character limits and spacers


Spacer characters are real characters. In a 150-character bio, three blank lines use three of them. The counter shows the total, and a badge turns red when you go over. Captions allow 2,200 characters, which is much more, so spacing costs little there. If your bio is tight, consider removing blank lines you do not need, or use a different separator, such as a bullet or a vertical bar, instead of vertical space.


How a caption is displayed


The feed shows only the first lines of a long caption, then a more link. About 125 characters are visible, depending on the device. The preview in this tool uses that limit when you pick the caption setting. Put the most important words first, because many people never tap more. Blank lines at the start of a caption push your hook further down, so avoid empty lines in the first line or two.


Accessibility and good practice


Invisible characters can be announced as blank by some screen readers, or skipped. Use them for visual separation only, and never place important words after them. Keep your caption readable if the spacing is lost, with clear sentences, and add image descriptions for accessibility. Avoid long runs of spacer lines.


Privacy, limits and responsible use


The tool works in your browser and does not store or send your text. It does not connect to Instagram and cannot post for you. Instagram updates its apps often, and text behavior may change. Test before you publish important content, follow Instagram's Community Guidelines, and disclose paid partnerships clearly.


When spacing does not work


If your blank lines still disappear, work through these steps. First, check that the option to fill blank lines is switched on and that the result box shows dots on those lines. Second, try another spacer character, because some apps trim one kind but not another. Third, paste the text in the app itself, not inside another editor that may remove special characters. Fourth, edit the bio from the app on your phone rather than from a browser, as the two can behave differently. Finally, check that the character count is still within the limit, because a bio that is too long is cut or rejected, and the spacers use up characters.
Some examples show how to use blank lines well. A bio might have three groups: who you are, what you offer and how to contact you, with one spacer line between each group, which costs only two characters. A caption might have a hook line, a blank line, three short lines of detail, another blank line and a question. For a list of services, avoid blank lines between items and use a bullet at the start of each line, so the list reads as one block.


Frequently asked questions


What is an Instagram line break generator?
A tool that keeps your blank lines in a bio or caption by filling them with an invisible character. This free one also moves hashtags below dots, collapses extra spacing and checks the character limit.

Why does Instagram remove my blank lines?
In bios and some caption fields, completely empty lines are trimmed. A line with an invisible character is not empty, so it stays.
What is the invisible character?
The default is the Braille blank, a Unicode character that takes space but shows nothing. Other options are the Hangul filler, an em space and a zero-width space. Braille blank works in most places.
Do the invisible characters count toward the limit?
Yes. Each one counts as a character, and the counter includes them. A bio allows 150 characters, so use blank lines sparingly there.
How many characters can an Instagram bio and caption have?
As we understand current rules, 150 for a bio and 2,200 for a caption. The tool shows both so that you can compare.
Is it safe for my account?
Yes. These are ordinary text characters. They do not break any rules, but check how they look on your device after posting.
Why does my text look different on another phone?
Fonts and apps handle special characters differently. Some devices may show a faint space or a box. Test on more than one device if the layout matters.
How do I hide hashtags in a caption?
Turn on Move hashtag-only lines below the text and choose a number of dot lines. The hashtags move to the end after the dots, and Instagram usually collapses long captions with a more link.
Does hiding hashtags help reach?

What does Blank line between every line do?

What does Collapse mean?
