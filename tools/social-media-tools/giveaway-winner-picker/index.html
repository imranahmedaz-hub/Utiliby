What this free giveaway winner picker does

Running a giveaway is easy. Proving that it was fair is harder. People who did not win want to know how the winner was chosen, and a transparent method protects your reputation. This free online giveaway winner picker draws winners from a list of names, usernames or comments using the browser's secure random generator, so that every entry has an equal chance. It removes duplicates, supports bonus entries, excludes accounts you name, draws backup winners at the same time and shows the pick with a short animation that works well in a live video.

After each draw it produces a record with the date, the number of entries, the winners, the backups, the full list and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the pool, which you can copy, download or print. Everything runs in your browser, and your list is not uploaded.

How to pick winners

  1. Collect your entries. Copy names from comments, a form or a spreadsheet, one per line.
  2. Paste them. The tool shows how many entries it found and how many duplicates it merged.
  3. Set the draw. Choose the number of winners and backups. Exclude your own account.
  4. Press Draw winners. A short animation runs, then the winners appear.
  5. Save the record. Copy, download or print the draw record.
  6. Contact the winners. Use the backups if someone is unavailable or does not qualify.

What makes a giveaway fair

Fairness comes from clear rules, a clean list and an honest draw. Publish the rules before you start: how to enter, the closing time, who is eligible, the prize and how you will choose and contact winners. Close the entries at the stated time and take a copy of the list at that moment. Remove ineligible entries before you draw, not after. Then draw once, with a method that you can explain. Random selection with a secure generator is that kind of method, and keeping the record means that you can show what you did.

How the random draw works

The tool counts tickets, picks a random whole number between zero and the total, and finds the entry whose ticket range contains it. It then removes that entry and repeats for the next winner, so nobody wins twice. The random number comes from the browser's cryptographic generator, and it uses rejection sampling, which means that every number is equally likely. It is not a pseudo-random sequence that anyone could reproduce from a seed. The fingerprint of the entry list lets you show later that the list was not changed.

Giveaways on social media

Each platform has promotion rules. Generally you must state clearly that the platform does not sponsor or endorse the promotion, you must not ask people to tag themselves in photos they are not in, and you must follow local law. Many platforms also forbid asking people to share a post to enter in a way that spams others. Winners should be contacted through official channels, and you should be careful about scam messages that pose as giveaway organizers. If you work with a brand, disclose the partnership clearly.

Legal basics

Rules for giveaways differ widely. Some countries regulate contests, sweepstakes and prize draws, and require official rules, a free route of entry, registration with an authority for larger prizes or limits on who can enter. Taxes may apply to prizes. Collect and use entrant data only as your privacy policy and law allow, and tell entrants what you will do with it. This tool is a random picker, not legal advice. For larger prizes or business promotions, ask a qualified professional.

Tips for a better giveaway

  • Keep entry simple. One or two steps get more entries than six.
  • Pick a relevant prize. A prize that fits your audience attracts the right followers.
  • Set a clear deadline. Give the date and time zone.
  • Announce the result. Post the winner and say how you chose them.
  • Check winners. Make sure they followed your rules.
  • Follow up. Confirm delivery of the prize.

Privacy, limits and responsible use

The picker runs in your browser and does not store or send your list. Names that you paste may be personal data, so use them only for the purpose that entrants agreed to. The tool cannot confirm that an entry is genuine, so bots, duplicates under different names and ineligible entrants are for you to detect. Keep the draw record with your rules in case of a question, and follow the laws and platform rules that apply to you.

Announcing winners and avoiding scams

Announce the result as soon as the draw is done, in the same place where people entered. Say how many entries there were, how you drew the winner and when, and show the draw or share the record. Contact winners through the platform's official messages and ask them to reply within a stated time, such as 48 hours, and move to the next backup if they do not. State clearly in your rules that you will never ask winners to pay a fee, share a password or send a code to receive a prize.

Scammers often copy real giveaways, create fake accounts with similar names and message entrants to say that they have won. Warn your audience about this in the post itself, and tell them which account name is the real one. Check that a winner is a genuine person, for example by looking at the age and activity of the account, if your rules allow it. Keep a checklist for every giveaway: published rules, closing time, eligibility checks, draw record, winner contact, prize delivery and a thank-you post. Having the same steps each time saves effort and keeps you fair.

Frequently asked questions

What is a giveaway winner picker?

A tool that chooses random winners from a list of entries. This free one handles duplicates, bonus entries, exclusions and backups, and saves a record of the draw.

How do I use this free giveaway winner picker online?

Paste your entries, one per line, set the number of winners and backups, and press Draw winners. The winners appear after a short animation, and you can copy, download or print the record.

Is the draw really random?

Yes. It uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator with rejection sampling, so every ticket has an equal chance and there is no modulo bias.

Can I pick winners from Instagram or TikTok comments?

Yes, if you copy the commenter names into the list. This tool does not connect to any platform, so you collect the names yourself. Check the platform's rules about giveaways and about using comment data.

How do I handle people who entered more than once?

Keep Count each person once switched on to merge duplicates, ignoring case and a leading @. Turn it off if each line should be a separate ticket.

How do bonus entries work?

Tick the option and write a number after a bar, such as anna_k | 3. That person gets three tickets in the draw. Use this only if your rules allow extra entries.

What are backup winners?

Extra names drawn at the same time, in order. If the first winner is not eligible or does not reply, you contact the next backup.

Can I exclude my own account or ineligible people?

Yes. Add their names to the exclude box. They are removed before the draw.

What is the pool fingerprint?

A SHA-256 hash of the sorted list of entries. If someone changes the list later, the fingerprint changes, so it helps show which list was used. It does not prove who was in a social media comment thread.

Can I show the draw live?

Yes. Share your screen and press Draw winners during a live video. The animation and result are visible to everyone.

Can I save proof of the draw?

Yes. Copy, download or print the record, which includes the date, the entries, the winners and the fingerprint. Keep it with your giveaway rules.

Does it check that entries are valid?

No. You must check that winners met your rules, such as following the account, tagging friends or living in an eligible country.

Are giveaways legal?

Rules vary by country and by prize. Many places regulate contests, sweepstakes and lotteries, and require clear terms. Some require a free way to enter or a registration. Ask a qualified professional if unsure.

What are the platform rules?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and others have promotion guidelines, such as that you must not imply the platform sponsors the giveaway and must state it clearly. Read the current rules.

Is my list uploaded?

No. It stays in your browser.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use online with no sign-up and no limit on draws.

How many entries can it handle?

Thousands of lines are fine on a modern device. Very large lists may slow the page slightly.
