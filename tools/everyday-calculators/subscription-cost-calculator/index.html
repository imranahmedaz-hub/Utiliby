What this free subscription cost calculator does

Subscriptions are designed to be easy to start and easy to forget. A few streaming plans, an app, a gym and a membership can quietly add up to hundreds of dollars a year. A subscription cost calculator brings them together in one place. This free online tool takes up to twelve recurring payments, with any billing cycle from weekly to yearly, and converts each into a monthly, yearly, five-year and ten-year cost.

A share chart shows which subscriptions take the biggest slice. Tick or untick Keep to see what cancelling would save, then see what that money could grow to if invested, and how much price rises would cost you. A third tab compares a monthly with an annual plan, with a break-even in months. If you wish, the tool can remember your list in your own browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use the subscription cost calculator

  1. Add each subscription with its name, price and billing cycle.
  2. Read the totals: per month, per year, per day, five years and ten years.
  3. Check the chart to see the biggest costs.
  4. Untick Keep on any you might cancel to see the savings.
  5. Use the second tab to see what the savings could grow to and the effect of price rises.
  6. Use the third tab to choose between a monthly and an annual plan.

How subscription costs are calculated

Every price is converted to a yearly cost: weekly prices are multiplied by 52, two-weekly by 26, monthly by 12, quarterly by 4, six-monthly by 2 and yearly by 1. Add them for the yearly total, then divide by 12 for the monthly figure. For video streaming at $15.49 a month, music at $11.99, cloud storage at $2.99, a gym at $40 and a $139 annual membership, the yearly total is $984.64, or $82.05 a month. Over five years that is $4,923.20 if no prices change.

Cancel, save and invest

Cancelling is the only sure way to cut a subscription cost. If you unticked the $40 gym, you would save $480 a year. If you instead put the $40 a month into an investment returning 7 percent a year, it could grow to about $6,842 in ten years from $4,800 of deposits. The what-if tab uses the money from every subscription you untick. Returns are never guaranteed, so treat the figure as an illustration of how small monthly amounts grow, not a forecast.

The cost of price rises

Subscription prices tend to rise over time. If the same $984.64 a year went up 5 percent a year, the ten-year total would be about $12,384 rather than $9,846 at flat prices. The tool shows that difference so you can judge whether a service still gives value as the bill creeps up. Look at the price each time you renew, and set a calendar reminder to review.

Monthly or annual billing

Annual plans often cost less per month, but they commit you for a year. Compare twelve monthly payments with the annual price. A plan at $15.49 a month costs $185.88 over twelve months. If the annual price is $155, you save $30.88, and the effective monthly price is $12.92. The break-even is $155 divided by $15.49, about 10 months. If you may stop sooner, monthly can be cheaper. If you will definitely keep it, annual wins.

How to find forgotten subscriptions

  • Scan bank and card statements for repeat charges from the last three months.
  • Check app store subscriptions on your phone and tablet.
  • Search your email for receipts, renewals and trial confirmations.
  • Review a password manager for old accounts.
  • Ask the household who signed up for what.

Tips for paying less

  • Rotate streaming services instead of paying for all at once.
  • Share family plans where the terms allow it.
  • Pick annual billing only for services you are sure to keep.
  • Set renewal reminders and cancel trials the day they start.
  • Review cost per use. A gym used twice a month is expensive.

Accuracy, privacy and responsible use

Results depend on the prices you enter. The tool does not connect to your accounts, so it cannot find charges for you or add taxes and currency fees. Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my total subscription cost?

Convert each subscription to a yearly cost and add them up. Monthly price x 12, weekly x 52, quarterly x 4, then divide by 12 for a monthly figure. The calculator does this for up to 12 subscriptions.

How much do subscriptions cost per year on average?

It varies widely by household. Adding common video, music, cloud, gym and shopping memberships can easily reach several hundred dollars a year, as the five-item example at $984.64 shows.

Why does a small monthly fee matter?

Monthly fees repeat. A $15.49 plan is $185.88 a year and $929.40 over five years, which is why seeing the long-run total helps decide what to keep.

How do I convert a weekly subscription to monthly?

Multiply the weekly price by 52 and divide by 12. A $5 weekly plan costs $21.67 a month.

How do I compare a monthly and an annual plan?

Multiply the monthly price by 12 and compare it with the annual price. If the annual plan costs less and you will use it for the full year, it saves money. The break-even is the annual price divided by the monthly price.

Is the annual plan always cheaper?

Usually per month, but only if you keep it long enough. At $15.49 monthly versus $155 yearly, you need to use it for about 10 months to break even.

How much would I save by cancelling a subscription?

Untick Keep next to it and the calculator shows the saving per month and per year across all the ones you cancel.

What happens if I invest the money I save?

The cancel and invest tab shows the future value of putting the monthly savings into an investment at a return you choose. Thirty dollars a month at 7 percent for ten years grows to about $5,132, from $3,600 of deposits.

Can it include price increases?

Yes. Enter a yearly price rise and the tool shows the total over your chosen number of years compared with flat prices.

How do I find all my subscriptions?

Check bank and card statements for repeat charges, app store subscriptions on your phone, email receipts, and your password manager. Many people find one or two they forgot.

Does it include free trials?

No. Enter a trial as a subscription at its paid price if you intend to continue after the trial ends, and set a reminder to cancel if not.

Can I save my list?

Tick Remember my list in this browser only. It is saved in your browser's local storage on this device, never sent anywhere, and you can untick to erase it.

How do I handle yearly and quarterly bills?

Pick the billing cycle for each line. The tool converts yearly, 6-month, quarterly, 2-weekly and weekly charges to a common monthly and yearly cost.

What counts as a subscription?

Any repeat charge: streaming, music, software, news, gyms, meal kits, boxes, memberships, apps, cloud storage and insurance add-ons.

What should I cancel first?

Start with the ones you use least relative to their cost. The share chart shows the biggest items, and cost per use is a good test.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. The remember option stores only in your own browser.

Is this subscription cost calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use online with no sign-up.
