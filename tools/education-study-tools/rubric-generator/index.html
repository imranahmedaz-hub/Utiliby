What is a free rubric generator online?

A free rubric generator online is a tool that creates a grading rubric from a template or from scratch. Instead of building a grid in a word processor and formatting every cell, you pick a template — essay, lab report, presentation, group project, discussion and more — and the tool fills in the criteria, performance levels and point values. You then edit any cell directly and print or copy the result.

This rubric generator goes beyond a static template library. It includes 12 templates covering the most common assignment types in K-12 and higher education. It supports 3, 4 or 5 performance levels. Every cell in the rubric is editable — you can rename criteria, rewrite descriptors and change point values. It prints cleanly to A4 or Letter without the tool UI appearing on the page. And it exports as plain text you can paste into Google Docs or Word. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up, no account, no upload, no tracking.

How to use this free rubric maker

  1. Enter your assignment title.

Analytic vs holistic vs single-point rubrics

TypeWhat it doesBest for
AnalyticScores each criterion separatelyMost assignments — gives specific feedback
HolisticGives one overall scoreQuick grading of large volumes
Single-pointDescribes only the target levelFormative feedback, self-assessment

Why rubrics improve grading

  • Consistency. A rubric standardises grading across multiple students, classes and graders.
  • Transparency. Students know exactly what is expected before they start.
  • Specific feedback. Instead of a single grade, students see which criteria they met and which they missed.
  • Faster grading. With the criteria predefined, grading becomes a matter of matching evidence to descriptors.
  • Fewer appeals. A rubric shows the basis of the grade, which reduces disputes.
