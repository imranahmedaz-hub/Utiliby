What this free words to pages calculator does

A teacher asks for a five-page essay, an editor wants 3,000 words and a form allows two pages. Word counts and page counts are different measures, and converting between them is guesswork unless you know the formatting. This free online words to pages calculator does the conversion for you. Enter a word count, or paste your text, choose the font, size, spacing, margins and paper, and see the number of pages. Change the settings and the answer updates immediately.

It works both ways. You can convert pages to words, see a table of common lengths in pages, and find the font size that would fit your text to a page limit. A drawing of the page shows your margins and the number of lines of text, so you can see why a change in spacing matters so much. Everything runs in your browser.

Rules of thumb for words per page

SetupWords per page
Single-spaced, 12 point, 1 inch marginsabout 500 to 600
1.5 spacingabout 400
Double-spaced, 12 point, 1 inch marginsabout 250 to 300

Teachers often quote 250 words per double-spaced page and 500 per single-spaced page, which is a convenient rule. Real documents usually come out slightly above that, because of the line fill and paragraph breaks. This tool models the layout directly, so you also see the effect of other fonts and margins. The result is an estimate, and your word processor remains the final judge.

What changes the page count

  • Line spacing: Doubling the spacing roughly halves the words per page.
  • Font and size: A one-point increase in size cuts the words per page by about 15 percent. Wide fonts such as Verdana fit fewer words than narrow ones such as Garamond.
  • Margins: Wider margins shrink the text area. Going from 1 inch to 1.25 inches costs about 10 percent.
  • Paper size: A4 is narrower and taller than US Letter.
  • Paragraphs: Extra spacing after paragraphs and short paragraphs add white space.
  • Headings, tables and images: These take room that words would fill. Add extra pages to allow for them.

Fitting text to a page limit

Sometimes a document runs a little over a limit. The fit tab calculates the font size that would make your words fill the limit, using the other settings. Treat it as information, not a shortcut. Instructors, courts and publishers often specify the font, size and margins exactly, and changing them to squeeze more words in breaks the rules and is easy to spot. A better approach is to edit. Remove repetition, shorten long sentences and cut material that does not support the main point. Use the word counter to track your progress.

Choosing fonts for documents

Times New Roman at 12 point is the traditional choice for academic work, with Arial and Calibri also common. APA 7 accepts several fonts, such as Times New Roman 12, Calibri 11 and Arial 11, and MLA suggests a readable font at 12 point. Courier is still used in screenplays and some legal documents because every character has the same width, which makes page counts predictable. When you do have a choice, pick a font that your readers find easy to read, not the one that gives the shortest page count.

Converting pages to words

The pages to words tab does the reverse. It multiplies the number of pages by the words per page. If a five-page paper is required, you will need about 1,500 words double-spaced or about 3,000 single-spaced in the standard setup. The tab also shows the single-spaced and double-spaced equivalents for your other settings.

Tips for hitting a page target

  • Plan the word count first, then write to it.
  • Check your page count in your word processor at the end.
  • Leave space for the title page, abstract and references.
  • Allow for headings, tables and figures.
  • Do not alter the margins or font size to cheat a limit.
  • Use the word counter and reading time tools to check the finished text.

Common mistakes

  • Using one rule for every document. Fonts and margins change the answer.
  • Forgetting extra pages. Title pages, abstracts and reference lists count toward some limits and not others.
  • Trusting the estimate over the document. Your word processor is the final check.
  • Padding text to fill pages. Readers notice filler.
  • Ignoring word limits that are set in words. Some courses set limits in words and some in pages.

Limits and privacy

The calculator models text as even lines of average English words. It cannot know your headings, images, tables, footnotes or the exact fonts installed on your computer, and languages with longer or shorter words will differ. Treat the result as a planning estimate. Your text is processed in your browser and is not uploaded or stored.

Frequently asked questions

How many words are on a page?

It depends on the font, size, spacing and margins. With Times New Roman 12 point, 1 inch margins and double spacing a page holds about 300 words, and single spaced about 600.

How many pages is 1,000 words?

About 3 to 4 pages double-spaced and about 2 pages single-spaced in a standard 12 point font with 1 inch margins.

How many pages is 500 words?

About 1.5 to 2 pages double-spaced, or a bit under one page single-spaced.

How many pages is 2,000 words?

About 6 to 7 pages double-spaced and about 3 to 4 pages single-spaced.

How many pages is 5,000 words?

About 16 to 17 pages double-spaced and about 8 to 9 pages single-spaced, in the standard setup.

How many words is a 5-page essay?

About 1,300 to 1,500 words double-spaced in Times New Roman 12. The pages to words tab calculates this for your own settings.

What affects the number of words per page?

Font and size, line spacing, margins, paper size, paragraph spacing, indents, headings, images and tables all change it. Fonts such as Verdana and Courier New take more room than Times New Roman.

Which font fits the most words on a page?

Narrow fonts such as Garamond and Times New Roman fit the most words, while wide fonts such as Verdana and Courier New fit the fewest at the same size.

Is a Word page the same as a Google Docs page?

Not exactly. Each program lays text out slightly differently, so page counts can differ. Check the final count in the program you are using.

Does this work for A4 paper?

Yes. Choose A4, US Letter, US Legal or A5. A4 is narrower and taller than US Letter, so the words per page differ a little.

How do I convert pages to words?

Use the pages to words tab. Enter the number of pages and it multiplies by the words per page for your settings.

What if my assignment has a page limit?

Use the fit to a limit tab to see the font size your word count would need, but follow the instructor's rules. Shrinking the font or margins to meet a limit is usually not allowed.

Does it include the title page and references?

Add them as extra pages. The total pages shown includes them.

How are words counted when I paste text?

The tool counts words the same way a word processor does, treating hyphenated words and contractions as one word.

How do I print or save only the results?

Use the print or save button. The printout has the results and the page drawing only, not the page layout of this site or the article. You can also copy the results or download a CSV.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to a server or saved.

Is this words to pages calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use online with no sign-up and no limit.
