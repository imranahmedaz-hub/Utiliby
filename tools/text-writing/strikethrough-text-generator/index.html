How strikethrough text works

Unicode includes combining marks. These are characters that have no width of their own and are drawn over the character before them. The strikethrough mark is U+0336, the Combining Long Stroke Overlay. Put it after a letter and the app draws a line through that letter. The tool adds the chosen mark after every character in your text.

Not every style is a true strikethrough. Diagonal slash draws slanted lines and wavy underline sits below the letters, so those two are better for decoration than for showing something has been deleted.

Where to use strikethrough text

  • Sales and discounts. Show a crossed-out original price next to the new price, as in $49 $29.
  • Bios and captions. Add a crossed-out phrase for a joke or a change of mind.
  • Posts and corrections. Show a correction while keeping the original wording visible.
  • Chat messages. Cross out a word to be funny or to show you changed your mind.
  • Lists and notes. Cross off finished items in a plain-text checklist.
  • Email subject lines. Use it sparingly for emphasis, and check how the subject looks in your mail app first.
  • Revision notes. Mark options you dropped in comments and notes.

Limitations of Unicode strikethrough

  • Apps and fonts draw it differently. The line may sit high, low, or break between letters, and a few fonts do not support some marks.
  • Screen readers may struggle. Combining marks can confuse assistive technology, so do not use generated strikethrough for information people must understand.
  • Search will not match it. A search for the plain word will not find the crossed-out version.
  • It is not real formatting. Spell check, word counts and editing tools may treat the text oddly.
  • Some fields reject it. Handles, usernames and some forms accept only plain letters and numbers.

Tips for better results

  • Use single strikethrough when you want the clearest, most widely drawn line.
  • Keep crossed-out text short. Long passages are hard to read.
  • Put the new text in plain letters next to the crossed-out text.
  • Test the pasted text in the app you plan to use before you publish.
  • Use the app's own strikethrough where it exists, especially for documents that others will edit.

What is a free strikethrough text generator online?

A free strikethrough text generator online is a tool that adds a line through text using Unicode combining marks. Because the line is built from characters and not from formatting, it can be pasted into many apps that have no strikethrough button.

How does strikethrough text work?

The tool adds the Unicode combining character U+0336 (Combining Long Stroke Overlay) after each character. How do I strikethrough text on WhatsApp?

WhatsApp has its own formatting: put a tilde on each side of the text, as in ~text~. Its help page for formatting messages describes this. You can also paste text from this generator, which works as ordinary characters.

How do I strikethrough text on Discord?

Discord supports Markdown, so put two tildes on each side of the text, as in ~~text~~. This only works in places where Discord renders Markdown, so for names and other fields that show plain text you can paste text from this generator instead.

How do I strikethrough text on Slack?

Slack lets you wrap text in a tilde on each side, as in ~text~, according to its help page on formatting messages with markup. Pasting generated text also works, though it may not look identical in every font.

How do I cross out text in Google Docs or Word?

In Google Docs, select the text and use Format, then Text, then Strikethrough. In Word, select the text and use the strikethrough button on the Home tab. Both apply real formatting, which is better than generated characters for documents that people will edit or search.

What is the difference between Unicode strikethrough and normal strikethrough?

Normal strikethrough is formatting applied to ordinary letters by an app. Unicode strikethrough puts extra characters after each letter. The first stays searchable and accessible, while the second works in more places but can confuse search, spell check, word counts and screen readers.

Why does the line look off-centre, broken or missing?

Each app and font draws combining marks in its own way. Some fonts shift the line up or down, some break it between letters and a few do not support a mark at all. Try another line style, or check how the text looks in the place where you will post it.

Can I strikethrough text on my phone?

Yes. This page works in a mobile browser. Type your text, choose a style, copy the result and paste it into any app. You do not need to install anything.

Is strikethrough text accessible to screen readers?

Not reliably. Screen readers may read each character with its mark, skip the marks or read the text oddly. Do not use generated strikethrough for information people need, such as a price change in a legal notice, and add a plain-text explanation next to it.

Will strikethrough text affect search or SEO?

Yes, it can. Because there are extra characters between the letters, a search for the plain word will not match the crossed-out version. Avoid using it for page titles, headings or key phrases on a website.

What do the different line styles look like?

Single strikethrough is the standard line through the text and short strikethrough is a shorter stroke. Diagonal slash draws slanted lines through the letters, wavy strikethrough draws a wavy line through them and wavy underline puts a wavy line below. Underline and overline add a line below or above. Exact looks depend on the font.

Can I cross out only part of my text?

Which style should I use for an old price or an edit?

Use single strikethrough. It is the closest match to the line people expect, and it is the style most fonts draw cleanly. For a price, write the old price crossed out and the new price in plain text next to it.
