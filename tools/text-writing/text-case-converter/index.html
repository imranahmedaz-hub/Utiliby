Everyday cases: upper, lower, sentence and capitalised

UPPER CASE turns every letter into a capital, useful for warnings, short labels and legal headings. lower case removes all capitals, which helps when normalising data, comparing strings or fixing text typed with caps lock on.

Sentence case capitalises the first letter of each sentence and the pronoun I, and lowercases the rest. It is the best fix for text that arrived in all capitals. The tool understands that a full stop after a number is not a sentence end and starts a new capital after each new line. Proper nouns are not detected, so names such as London need a quick manual edit.

Capitalise Each Word makes the first letter of every word a capital, including small words such as of and the. It is a quick format for names, product lists and labels, but it is not correct title case for headlines.

URL slugs

A slug is the readable part of a web address, such as /text-case-converter/. A good slug is short, lowercase, uses hyphens between words and avoids accents, symbols and filler words. The URL slug style does the mechanical part for you: it lowercases text, removes accents and punctuation and joins the words with hyphens. Trim the result by hand to the few words that matter, because short slugs are easier to read and share.

Alternating and inverse case

aLtErNaTiNg cAsE switches between lowercase and uppercase on every letter and is mostly used for tone in social posts. iNVERSE cASE flips each letter, so capitals become lowercase and lowercase become capitals. Inverse case can also repair text typed with caps lock on, where every shift press produces the opposite of what was intended.

Protecting acronyms and brand names

The most common problem with automatic case conversion is damage to words that must keep their spelling. NASA must not become Nasa and iPhone must not become Iphone. The tool offers two protections. Keep all-caps acronyms leaves words that are entirely capitals alone. Keep mixed-case brands leaves words with an internal capital letter or digit alone in title case. Both are enabled by default. Words that only look ordinary in lowercase, such as a product name typed in lowercase, cannot be detected, so proofread names after converting.

Turkish and Azerbaijani letters

In English, i and I are the same letter in two cases. Turkish and Azerbaijani have both a dotted and a dotless i, so the capital of i is the dotted İ and the lowercase of I is the dotless ı. Applying English rules produces wrong text, a long-standing source of bugs in software. Choose the language rule if you convert upper or lower case text in either language and the result will follow local convention.

Cleaning spaces and line breaks

Text copied from PDFs, emails and websites often arrives with repeated spaces, trailing spaces and odd blank lines. The tidy option collapses repeated spaces, trims line ends and reduces blank lines to one. Join lines removes line breaks and replaces them with a single space, which is useful for text copied from a column or a hard-wrapped email. Both options are applied before the case conversion.

Tips for cleaner text

  • Fix all-caps text with sentence case, then correct names by hand.
  • Use title case only for headings, and use the same style throughout a document.
  • Prefer sentence case for interface text and subheadings. It is easier to read.
  • Convert variable names with the programming styles instead of retyping.
  • Check the highlighted strip if you worry that the tool changed too much.
  • Chain conversions with Use result as input.

Common mistakes

  • Using capitalise each word for headlines. It capitalises small words that style guides keep lowercase.
  • Mixing title styles. Pick one style for the whole document.
  • Converting names. Case tools cannot know whether a word is a proper noun.
  • Typing long text in all capitals. It is slower to read and often seen as shouting.
  • Ignoring accents in code. Strip them where the platform requires plain letters.

Frequently asked questions

What is a text case converter?

A text case converter changes the capitalisation of text. It can make text uppercase, lowercase, sentence case or title case, and convert it to programming styles such as camelCase and snake_case.

How do I change the case of text online?

Paste your text, then choose a style. The converted text appears straight away and you can copy it, download it or print it.

What is the difference between sentence case and title case?

Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence and proper nouns, as in normal writing. Title case capitalises the main words of a heading or title, and the rules depend on the style guide.

Which title case style should I use?

Use the style your publisher, school or employer requires. AP is common in journalism, APA in social science papers, Chicago in books and the humanities, and MLA in language and literature courses.

How do the title case styles differ?

Mainly in which short words stay lowercase. AP and APA capitalise longer prepositions such as with and from, while Chicago and MLA lowercase every preposition. APA also capitalises words of four letters or more.

Does it keep acronyms such as NASA in capitals?

Yes, when the option to keep all-caps acronyms is on. Words already written in capitals are left alone in title case, capitalise-each-word and sentence case.

What happens to brand names like iPhone or eBay?

With the mixed-case option on, words that already contain an internal capital letter or digit are left unchanged in title case, so iPhone stays iPhone. Proper nouns in other words may still need a manual check.

What is camelCase, PascalCase and snake_case?

They are naming conventions used in programming. camelCase starts lowercase and capitalises each later word, PascalCase capitalises every word, and snake_case joins lowercase words with underscores.

What is kebab-case and where is it used?

kebab-case joins lowercase words with hyphens. It is common in URLs, CSS class names and file names.

Can I create a URL slug?

Yes. The URL slug style lowercases the text, removes accents and punctuation and joins words with hyphens, which suits page addresses.

What is alternating case and inverse case?

Alternating case switches between lowercase and uppercase on every letter, which is used for jokes and emphasis. Inverse case flips each letter, turning capitals into lowercase and the reverse.

Why is there a Turkish and Azerbaijani option?

In these languages the letter i has a dotted and a dotless form, so uppercase i becomes a dotted capital and I becomes a dotless lowercase. The language option applies these rules to upper and lower case.

Can it clean up extra spaces and line breaks?

Yes. Tick the cleaning option to collapse repeated spaces and blank lines, or join lines to make a single line.

Does it keep my paragraphs and line breaks?

Yes. Line breaks are kept unless you choose to join lines. Programming styles are applied to each line separately.

How do I print or save only the converted text?

Use the print or save button. The printout has the original and converted text only, not the page layout or the article. You can also copy the result or download it as a text file.

Is my text uploaded or stored?

No. The conversion runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server or saved.

Is this case converter free?

Yes. It is free to use online with no sign-up and no limit.
