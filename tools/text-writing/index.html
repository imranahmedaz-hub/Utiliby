Text & Writing Tools

Free tools for generating styled Unicode text, fancy fonts and copy-paste decorations for bios, usernames and social media. Every tool runs in your browser, so nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere.

Bold Italic Text GeneratorConvert plain text into 32+ bold, italic, cursive and fancy Unicode styles you can copy and paste anywhere. Upside-Down Text GeneratorFlip, mirror and reverse text into Unicode. Multiple modes, live preview, one-click copy. Strikethrough Text GeneratorCross out text with Unicode. Six line styles, works on Instagram, X, Discord and Google Docs. Old English Font GeneratorConvert text to Gothic, Blackletter and Fraktur Unicode for bios, usernames and designs. Text to ASCII Art GeneratorFIGlet banners for README files, code comments and terminals. Eleven fonts, live preview. Word CounterCount words, characters, sentences and paragraphs, with keyword density, reading level, goal tracker and platform limits. Grammar CheckerCheck spelling, grammar, punctuation, wordiness and style with one-click fixes, US or UK English and a style report. Citation GeneratorCreate APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard and IEEE citations for books, websites, articles and more, and build a bibliography. Text Compare ToolCompare two texts and see added, removed and changed words, lines or characters with a similarity score. Lorem Ipsum GeneratorGenerate lorem ipsum or themed placeholder text by paragraphs, sentences, words or characters as text, HTML or Markdown. Text Case ConverterConvert text to upper, lower, sentence, AP, APA, Chicago and MLA title case, camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case and more. Reading Time CalculatorEstimate reading and speaking time, convert words to minutes and plan the word count and slides of a speech. Words to Pages CalculatorConvert words to pages or pages to words for any font, spacing, margins and paper size, and fit a page limit. Syllable CounterCount syllables in words, poems and lyrics line by line, see word splits and check haiku, tanka and limerick patterns.
