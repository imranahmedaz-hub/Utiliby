Cooking measurement conversions explained

Volume conversions

Volume measures how much space an ingredient occupies. Common volume units include teaspoons (tsp), tablespoons (tbsp), cups, fluid ounces (fl oz), millilitres (ml), litres (l), pints, quarts and gallons. Volume-to-volume conversions use fixed mathematical ratios. For example, 1 cup = 16 tablespoons = 48 teaspoons = 8 fluid ounces.

Weight conversions

Weight measures how heavy an ingredient is. Common weight units include grams (g), kilograms (kg), ounces (oz) and pounds (lb). Weight-to-weight conversions are also fixed: 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams, 1 pound = 16 ounces = 453.592 grams.

Volume-to-weight conversions

This is where ingredient density matters. A cup of flour and a cup of sugar occupy the same volume but weigh different amounts. Flour weighs about 125 grams per cup, sugar about 200 grams, brown sugar about 220 grams, honey about 340 grams. This converter uses ingredient-specific densities so your conversions are accurate.

Why ingredient density matters

One cup of flour does not weigh the same as one cup of sugar, honey, rice, cocoa or nuts. A generic converter that treats every cup the same produces errors of 40 to 170 percent when converting between volume and weight. This is why professional bakers weigh ingredients rather than using cup measures.

The spoon-and-level method — spooning flour into a cup, then levelling with a knife — is the standard technique that the density values in this converter assume. Scooping flour directly from the bag compacts it, adding up to 30 grams per cup. That single measuring error can add 25 percent more flour to a recipe, resulting in dry, dense baked goods.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in a cup of flour?

One US cup of all-purpose flour weighs approximately 125 grams when measured using the spoon-and-level method. Scooping directly from the bag compacts the flour and can add up to 30 extra grams per cup.

How many grams are in a cup of sugar?

One US cup of granulated sugar weighs approximately 200 grams. Brown sugar weighs about 220 grams per cup when packed.

Why is a cup of flour not the same weight as a cup of sugar?

Cups measure volume, while grams measure weight. A cup of flour and a cup of sugar occupy the same space but have different densities, so they weigh different amounts. Flour is less dense than sugar.

What is the difference between a US cup and a metric cup?

A US customary cup holds approximately 236.6 millilitres, while a metric cup used in many countries holds exactly 250 millilitres. This calculator lets you switch between both standards.

How do I convert oven temperatures?

Use the Temperature mode in this converter. Enter a value in Celsius or Fahrenheit and the tool shows the equivalent in both scales, plus the corresponding Gas Mark for UK recipes.

How accurate are ingredient weight conversions?

Ingredient conversions are accurate estimates based on standard density values. Actual weights vary with packing, grind size, moisture, brand and measuring technique. For precision baking, weighing ingredients directly is always recommended.
