Recipe & Cooking Tools

Free online tools for recipes, cooking times, conversions, nutrition and costing. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up, no tracking.

Cooking Measurement ConverterConvert cups, tablespoons, grams, ounces and millilitres for recipes and baking. Recipe Nutrition CalculatorCalories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre and sodium per serving from any pasted ingredient list, with % daily values and a food database. Meat & Turkey Cooking Time CalculatorRoasting time by weight and oven temperature for turkey, chicken, beef, pork, lamb and ham, plus thaw time and a plan from dinner time. Air Fryer Conversion CalculatorConvert oven recipes to the air fryer and back with new temperature and time, plus a cooking times chart for 76 foods. Recipe Cost CalculatorCost per serving, food cost percentage and menu price from ingredient prices, with unit conversion, yield, labour and overhead. Pizza Dough CalculatorFlour, water, salt and yeast by pizza size and style, with baker's percentages, cold ferment plan and a schedule from dinner. Nutrition Facts Label GeneratorCreate an FDA-style Nutrition Facts label with rounding and % Daily Values, and download it as a PNG or SVG. Baking Pan Size ConverterSwap cake and baking pans, scale the recipe, and adjust bake time and temperature for 28 common pan sizes. Rice to Water Ratio CalculatorWater for 20 types of rice and grains on the stove, rice cooker, Instant Pot or oven, with cook times and steps. Yeast Conversion CalculatorConvert instant, active dry and fresh yeast to grams, teaspoons and packets, and find yeast per flour weight and rise time.
