What this free online email subject line generator does

The subject line is the first thing a reader sees and often the only thing they read. It decides whether your email is opened, ignored or deleted. Writing a good one is hard when you have to do it every week, so most writers end up repeating the same few ideas. This free online email subject line generator gives you a fresh set of options in seconds. Enter your topic, choose a goal such as a newsletter, sale, welcome email, abandoned cart, event invitation, webinar, product launch, cold email, follow-up, win-back, thank you, survey or announcement, pick a tone, and the tool builds scored subject lines you can copy, save and preview.

Every line shows its length in characters, a score and warnings for common mistakes such as spam trigger words, ALL CAPS, too many exclamation marks or a line that is too long for phones. An inbox preview shows how the line and preheader look on a phone and on a desktop, and the tool suggests preheader text to go with it.

How to write email subject lines step by step

  1. Say what the email is about in a few words, such as spring collection.
  2. Choose the goal and the tone that fits your brand.
  3. Add details such as a discount or a deadline if you have one.
  4. Read the ideas. Lines that match your tone come first.
  5. Check the score and warnings. Fix long or spammy lines.
  6. Open the inbox preview to see how a line is cut off on a phone.
  7. Save your favorites and copy them into your email platform.
  8. Test two versions with a small part of your list.

Subject line ideas for every goal

  • Newsletter: promise a clear benefit or a number, such as 5 things to know about spring trends.
  • Sale: state the offer and the deadline, such as Save 20%, ends Sunday.
  • Welcome: be warm and say what happens next.
  • Abandoned cart: remind and help, such as Still thinking about it?
  • Event or webinar: include the name and a reason to attend, and say whether it is free.
  • Cold email: be short, specific and human.
  • Follow-up: refer to the earlier message and add a reason to reply.
  • Win-back: acknowledge the gap, and offer something new or an easy way to opt out.
  • Survey: mention how long it takes.

Principles of subject lines that work

  • Be clear before clever. People scan. A line that says what is inside beats a pun.
  • Front-load the important words. The first 40 characters matter most on a phone.
  • Be specific. A number, name or date is more believable than a vague promise.
  • Create real urgency only. Deadlines should be true. Fake countdowns cost you trust and can hurt deliverability.
  • Match the subject to the email. A subject that misleads can break the law and brings complaints.
  • Use sentence case. It is easier to read than all capitals or title case.
  • Write for one person. Use you and your.
  • Pair the subject with a preheader. They work as a unit.

How to A/B test subject lines

Pick two lines that differ in one way only, such as a question against a statement, or a number against no number. Send each to a random slice of your list, for example 10 percent each, and wait long enough to collect enough opens, usually a few hours. Compare opens and, more importantly, clicks. Send the winner to the rest and write down what you learned. Opens can be inflated by privacy features in some mail apps, so clicks and conversions are a better measure of success.

Honesty and the law

A subject line must not deceive. In the United States, the CAN-SPAM Act forbids misleading subject lines. Many other countries apply similar rules, and the European Union and United Kingdom also require a legal basis, usually consent, for marketing to individuals. This tool writes text only and is not legal advice.

Quick glossary

  • Preheader: the preview line shown after the subject in many inboxes.
  • Open rate: the share of recipients who open a message. Privacy features in some mail apps inflate it.
  • Click-through rate: the share of recipients who click a link.
  • Merge tag: a placeholder such as a first name that your email platform fills in for each person.
  • Segment: a part of your list that gets a tailored message.
  • A/B test: sending two versions to small groups to see which does better.
  • Win-back: a campaign for people who stopped engaging.
  • Sender name: the name shown next to the subject, which matters as much as the subject.

Accuracy, limits and responsible use

The ideas come from patterns and the score comes from simple rules, so the output is a starting point. The tool does not know your audience, brand voice or past results. Edit the lines, and always check that a subject reflects the content of the email.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this free email subject line generator?

Enter what your email is about, choose a goal and a tone, add a discount or deadline if you have one, and read the ideas. Save the best ones, preview them in an inbox and copy them.

Is this subject line generator really free?

Yes. It is a free online tool with no sign-up and no limits. Everything is made in your browser.

What is a good email subject line length?

Between about 25 and 50 characters is a common target. Phones show roughly 40 characters, so put the key words early. The tool shows a length meter and warns about lines that are too short or too long.

How many characters can an email subject line have?

There is no strict limit, but most inboxes show 30 to 70 characters, and anything after that is cut off. Keep the main message inside the first 40.

What makes a good subject line?

It is clear, honest, specific and relevant, it puts the key words first and gives a reason to open. Numbers, questions and a hint of urgency can help when they are true.

Do emojis help in subject lines?

Sometimes. A single relevant emoji can make a line stand out, but it can also look spammy or render badly in some programs. Test it with your audience. The tool lets you add one to some lines.

Should I personalize subject lines?

Using a first name can help in some cases, but it works best when the data is clean. If names are missing or wrong it backfires, so set a fallback in your email platform. The tool supports the merge tag style of common platforms.

What are spam trigger words?

Words such as free, guarantee, act now and winner are often found in spam, so they can add to a spam score. They do not guarantee a message goes to spam, but the tool flags them so you can choose another way to say it.

Does the score predict open rates?

No. The score is a rule-based guide about length and wording. Real open rates depend on your sender reputation, your audience and the content, so run A/B tests.

What is preheader text?

The preheader is the preview line that appears after the subject in many inboxes. It extends the subject, so write it as a second sentence. The tool suggests a few for your goal.

How do I A/B test subject lines?

Save two lines, send each to a small random share of your list, and compare opens or clicks after a set time. Send the winner to the rest. Change only one thing between versions.

What is the best subject line for a sale?

Say what is on offer and when it ends, for example Save 20% on spring collection, today only. Be specific and honest about the deadline.

What is a good subject line for a cold email?

Short, plain and relevant, such as Quick question about your onboarding. Avoid hype and write like a person.

What is a good subject line for a follow-up email?

A short line that refers to the earlier message, such as Following up on our call. Avoid pressure, and add new value when you can.

Should I use all capital letters?

No. All caps looks like shouting, can trigger spam filters and is harder to read. Use sentence case.

Are there legal rules for subject lines?

Yes. For example, CAN-SPAM requires that a subject line does not mislead about the content of the message. Other countries have similar rules.

Can I save or print my favorite lines?

Yes. Press Save on a line, then copy the saved list, download it as a text file, or print or save a one-page list.
