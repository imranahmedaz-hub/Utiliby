What this free online out of office message generator does

An out of office message, also called an automatic reply, auto-responder or vacation responder, tells people who write to you that you are away and what to do next. A good one saves you from a flood of follow-ups, tells customers who can help, and makes your company look organised. A poor one is vague, too long or reveals more than it should. This free online out of office message generator writes a clear draft in a few seconds, in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese or Italian, in a formal, friendly or short tone.

Choose a reason (vacation, holiday, business travel, a conference, training, parental leave, an extended leave, short absence or having left the company), add your dates and a backup contact and you get a subject line, a full out of office email, a one-line short version and an HTML version you can copy. The message is checked for common mistakes such as a return date before the last day away or an email address that does not look right.

How to write an out of office message step by step

  1. Choose the reason and tone. Formal suits clients and managers. Friendly suits colleagues and small businesses. Short and direct suits busy inboxes.
  2. Add your name, title and company. They appear in the signature.
  3. Set the dates. The first and last day away are optional. The return date appears in the subject line.
  4. Say how you handle email. Limited access, no access or occasional checks.
  5. Say when you will reply. On your return or within a number of business days.
  6. Name a backup. Add a colleague, an email address and a phone number for urgent matters.
  7. Copy it into your email program. The How to set it up tab lists Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and Yahoo.
  8. Test it. Ask a colleague to email you from another account.

Keep the whole message to a few short paragraphs. Nobody reads a long automatic reply, and the point is to send people to the right place.

Out of office messages for different situations

  • Vacation: give the return date, say you have limited access and name a colleague.
  • Public holiday or company closure: give the date the office reopens. A simple message is enough.
  • Business travel: say you are traveling, that replies may be slow and who can help.
  • Conference or training: mention the event if it is not confidential, and that you will reply when you return.
  • Parental or extended leave: keep it brief. Say you are on leave, whether you will reply and who is covering.
  • Short absence: a half-day in meetings can use a very short message with a time you will reply.
  • Leaving the company: say you are no longer there, that the address is not monitored and who to contact instead. Check with your employer about how long the address stays active.

Privacy and security of automatic replies

An automatic reply goes to every sender, and that includes spammers, scammers and strangers. Avoid giving the exact place you are going, the dates you are away from home, your personal phone number or details about your health or family. Scammers read automatic replies to learn who is away, who covers for you and how your organisation works. A single return date and a backup contact are usually enough. If a message is aimed at customers, you can also point them to a help page, which you can build quickly with the Mailto Link Generator.

Choosing the right tone and language

The formal tone uses full sentences and a traditional closing. The friendly tone sounds warmer and uses contractions in English. The short tone says only what is necessary. For international teams, write the same message in two languages in your signature block or choose the language of the people who write to you most. The generator formats dates for the language you select, so a Spanish message says lunes, 20 de octubre and a German one says Montag, 20. Oktober. Pair this tool with a clear email subject line for the follow-up you plan to send on your return.

How to turn on your automatic reply

Every email program has its own menu, but the idea is the same: turn on the reply, set a start and end date, add a subject and a message, and save. In Gmail it is called the vacation responder. In Outlook it is called automatic replies. Exchange and Microsoft 365 admins can set a reply for you. Remember to turn it off when you return if you did not set an end date. If your reply is sent as formatted mail, you can paste the HTML version from this tool. Common mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting the return date or giving a date that has already passed.
  • Giving too much personal information.
  • Promising to read email when you will not.
  • Naming a backup who does not know they were named.
  • Writing a very long message.
  • Leaving the reply on after you return.
  • Using the reply while you are on a mailing list that will answer back to every post.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write an out of office message?

State that you are away, give the dates or your return date, say how quickly you will reply, name a backup contact for urgent matters and sign off politely. This free generator builds that structure for you in six languages.

Is this out of office message generator really free?

Yes. It is a free online tool with no sign-up and no limits. Your details stay in your browser.

What should an out of office message include?

Your return date, whether you can read email, when people can expect a reply, who to contact for urgent matters, and a polite closing. Keep it short.

What is a good out of office subject line?

Something clear that starts with the status, such as Out of Office: back on Monday, October 20. The generator writes one that matches your dates.

Should I put my dates in the message?

Giving the return date is useful. Giving every travel date is not always wise, because the message reaches everyone who writes to you, including strangers. Many people only give the return date.

Should I share my phone number or travel plans?

Share a colleague's details, not your own, and avoid saying exactly where you are or that your home is empty. Treat an automatic reply as public.

How do I set up an out of office reply in Gmail?

Open Settings, choose See all settings, scroll to Vacation responder, turn it on, set the first and last day, add a subject and message and save. The How to set it up tab lists the steps for other mail programs.

How do I set up automatic replies in Outlook?

In desktop Outlook choose File, then Automatic Replies. In Outlook on the web or Microsoft 365 open Settings, Mail, Automatic replies. Turn it on, set the time range and paste your message.

Can I write a different message for people outside my company?

Yes. Outlook lets you set separate replies for people inside and outside your organisation. Use a shorter, more general message for outside senders.

What is a professional out of office message for vacation?

Thank the sender, say you are on vacation until a date, mention limited access to email, say you will reply on your return and give a colleague for urgent matters. Choose the Formal tone for this.

What should I write if I have left the company?

Say you no longer work there, say the address is not monitored and point to a person or shared mailbox. Choose I have left the company in the reason menu.

What should I write for maternity or parental leave?

Say you are on leave until a date or until further notice, that you will not reply, and who is covering your work. You do not need to give details.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian. Dates are written in the format of the language you pick.

Can I get a shorter message?

Yes. Choose the Short and direct tone, or use the short version that appears under the main message. It is useful for phone mail apps and chat status lines.

Can I paste the message into HTML email?

Yes. Copy as HTML gives you simple paragraph tags that you can paste into mail programs that support formatted replies.

Does the tool turn on my automatic reply?

No. It writes the text. You paste it into your email program, which sends the replies.

Can I print or save the message?

Yes. Download it as a text file, or print or save a one-page copy that shows only the message.
