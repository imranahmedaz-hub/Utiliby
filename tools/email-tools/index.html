Email Tools

Free online email tools: validate and clean lists, trace and analyze message headers, build DMARC and SPF records, write subject lines and out of office replies, create HTML email templates and mailto links, extract addresses and check your wording for spam triggers. Everything runs in your browser, with no sign-up and no tracking.

Email Validator & List CleanerCheck bulk email lists for invalid, duplicate, typo, role-based and disposable addresses, fix mistakes like gmial.com and download a clean list. Out of Office Message GeneratorWrite a professional automatic reply for vacation, travel or leave with subject, dates and a backup contact, in six languages and three tones. HTML Email Template BuilderBuild a responsive HTML email from blocks, preview it on desktop and mobile, run checks and copy clean table-based code. Email Header AnalyzerPaste raw headers to trace the delivery path, see delays at each hop, read SPF, DKIM and DMARC results and spot spoofing. DMARC & SPF Record GeneratorBuild valid DMARC and SPF records, see the DNS entry to publish, keep within the SPF lookup limit and check an existing record. Email Subject Line GeneratorGet scored subject lines for 13 goals and five tones, preview them in a phone and desktop inbox and copy preheader ideas. Mailto Link GeneratorBuild an encoded mailto link with subject, body, Cc and Bcc, copy it as HTML, a button or Markdown, and decode existing links. Email ExtractorPaste text or upload files to find every email address, including hidden forms, remove duplicates, filter by domain and export a list. Email Spam Word CheckerFind spam trigger words and risky structure in your email, see a risk score, highlighted text and safer wording ideas.
