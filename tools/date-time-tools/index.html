Date, Time & Scheduling Tools

Free online tools for dates, times, lunar phases and scheduling. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up, no tracking.

Moon Phase CalculatorFind the moon phase, illumination, moon age and upcoming full moons for any date. Includes lunar calendar and seasonal moon names. Shift Schedule GeneratorBuild 2-2-3 Pitman, 2-3-2, 4 on 4 off, 3 on 3 off, DuPont or custom rotating rosters. Check 24/7 coverage, print and export to calendar. Calendar Event ICS File GeneratorCreate .ics calendar files and Add to Google Calendar, Outlook and Yahoo links with time zones, repeats, reminders and bulk events. Time Card CalculatorTotal weekly or biweekly hours with breaks, overnight shifts, rounding and overtime. Convert minutes to decimal hours and print a timesheet. Meeting Time Zone PlannerCompare up to eight cities on a colour-coded timeline, find overlapping work hours and share the plan or add it to your calendar. Working Days CalculatorCount business days between dates, add or subtract working days and see public holidays for 17 countries with custom weekends.
