Audio & Music Tools

Free online tools to convert, cut, record, boost and analyze audio. Files are processed in your browser, never uploaded to a server — no sign-up.

MP4 to MP3 ConverterExtract audio from MP4, MOV and WebM video and save MP3 or WAV. Audio CutterCut and trim audio on a waveform, add fades and make ringtones. Vocal RemoverReduce or isolate vocals to make karaoke and instrumental versions. Audio Speed and Pitch ChangerChange tempo and pitch separately or together, with a BPM target. Online Voice RecorderRecord from your microphone, trim and save as MP3 or WAV. Audio Volume BoosterBoost or normalize loudness with a peak limiter and before/after view. BPM Detector and Tap TempoTap a beat or analyze a file to find BPM, beat grid and delay times. Song Key FinderDetect the musical key of a track with Camelot and Open Key codes. Silence RemoverRemove or shorten silent gaps in recordings and podcasts. Audio MergerJoin several audio files into one MP3 or WAV in your browser.
