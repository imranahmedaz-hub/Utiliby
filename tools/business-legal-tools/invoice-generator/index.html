What a compliant invoice should include

Invoice requirements vary by country and by whether you are registered for VAT or GST. In most jurisdictions, a compliant invoice must include the following:

ElementWhy it matters
The word "Invoice"Distinguishes it from a receipt, quote or statement
A unique invoice numberRequired for accounting and tax records; must be sequential and not reused
Your business name, address and contact detailsIdentifies the supplier
Your tax or VAT registration numberRequired for VAT reclaim in most jurisdictions
Client's name and addressIdentifies the customer
Invoice date and due dateStarts the payment clock and the VAT accounting period
Itemised goods or servicesDescription, quantity, unit price and line total
Subtotal, tax and totalShows how the amount due was calculated
Payment terms and instructionsTells the client how and when to pay

If you are VAT-registered in the EU or UK, your invoice must also show the VAT rate applied and the VAT amount separately. For cross-border transactions, the customer's VAT number may be required to apply the reverse-charge mechanism.

Invoice numbering best practices

Invoice numbers must be unique and sequential. Most accounting systems generate them automatically. A common format is a prefix followed by a zero-padded number: INV-0001, INV-0002, and so on. If you issue invoices from multiple systems or entities, use a distinct prefix for each. Never reuse an invoice number, even if you cancel the invoice — instead, issue a credit note that references the original.

Payment terms explained

Payment terms define when the invoice becomes due. The most common terms are:

  • Net 14 / Net 30 / Net 45 — payment is due 14, 30 or 45 days after the invoice date.
  • Due on receipt — payment is due immediately.
  • 2/10 Net 30 — the client gets a 2% discount if they pay within 10 days, otherwise the full amount is due in 30 days.

Shorter terms improve cash flow but may be resisted by larger clients. If you are a freelancer or small business, Net 14 is a reasonable default; Net 30 is the most common in B2B transactions.
