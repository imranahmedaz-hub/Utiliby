What this free online invoice generator does

Sending a clear invoice is the quickest way to get paid. Yet most free invoice tools put a watermark on the result, limit you to a few invoices, or ask you to create an account and then store your client list on their servers. This free online invoice generator does none of that. You fill in a form, see the invoice build in front of you, and print or save a clean PDF. Nothing is uploaded.

It handles the details that make an invoice correct: a sequential number, dates and payment terms that set the due date for you, line items with a taxable switch, tax that can be added on top or already included, a discount as a percentage or a fixed amount, shipping, deposits and a balance due. You can add your logo, bank details, a late fee notice, a signature line and a status stamp. The same tool makes quotes, proforma invoices and credit notes.

Freelancers, consultants, tradespeople, agencies and small shops can save their business details on their own device, save a full draft as a file, download the invoice as an HTML file, export the items as a spreadsheet file and add the due date to their calendar with reminders. Print gives only the invoice, never the page around it.

In the EU and UK, a VAT invoice must also show the VAT rate and amount, and for cross-border business customers the buyer's VAT number and a reverse-charge note may be needed. Rules differ elsewhere, so check yours.

Invoice numbering best practices

Numbers must be unique and should run in sequence. A prefix and a padded number, such as INV-0001, is clear and easy to sort. If you issue invoices from more than one business or system, use a different prefix for each. Never reuse a number, even if you cancel an invoice. Issue a credit note that refers to the original, and keep both.

Payment terms explained

  • Due on receipt. Payment is expected straight away.
  • Net 7, Net 14, Net 30, Net 45, Net 60. Payment is due that many days after the invoice date.
  • Early payment discounts such as 2/10 Net 30 give a 2% discount for payment within ten days. Put the wording in the notes.

Shorter terms help cash flow but large clients may push back. For small suppliers, Net 14 is a sensible start and Net 30 is the usual business-to-business term. A late fee notice, where the law allows it, encourages on-time payment.

How tax, discounts and deposits are calculated

The tool adds up your line amounts. If your prices already include tax, it first removes the tax from each taxable line. A percentage discount is then taken from the subtotal and the tax is worked out on what remains, only for lines marked taxable. Shipping is added after tax. Any deposit or payment already received is subtracted to give the balance due. Amounts are rounded to two decimals at the end.

Tips to get paid faster

  • Send the invoice the day the work is done.
  • Put the due date and the amount in the email subject.
  • Include bank details and a reference the client can quote.
  • Agree terms in writing before the work starts.
  • Set a calendar reminder for the due date and follow up the next day.
  • Ask for a deposit on larger jobs.

Why many free invoice tools are not really free

Common limits include a cap of a few invoices, a watermark until you upgrade, a mandatory account that is used for marketing, and client data stored on the vendor's servers, which can raise privacy questions. This generator works in your browser, stores nothing on a server and does not add a watermark. It includes a small credit line that you can switch off.

Common invoicing mistakes

  • Missing or duplicate invoice numbers.
  • Leaving out the tax number when you are registered.
  • Vague descriptions that invite questions.
  • No due date or payment details.
  • Applying tax to exempt items.
  • Sending an invoice with the wrong client name or address.

Tips for getting paid faster

  • Invoice immediately after delivery, not at month end.
  • State the due date and payment terms clearly, and add bank details or a payment link.
  • Reference a purchase order if the client uses one, so accounts payable can match it.
  • Offer an early-payment discount only if your margin allows.
  • Send reminders before and after the due date. The calendar reminder in this tool helps.
  • State late fees in advance, and check what your local law allows.

Frequently asked questions

What is a free invoice generator online?

A tool that builds a professional invoice in your browser. You enter your details, your client's details and the items, and it adds up the tax and total and gives you a printable invoice.

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on how many invoices you create. Your data stays in your browser.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Print or save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer in your browser's print window. Only the invoice is printed, not the web page around it.

What should an invoice include?

The word invoice, a unique number, your name and address, your tax number if registered, the client's name and address, the date and due date, itemised goods or services, tax, the total, and payment details.

How do I number my invoices?

Use a unique, sequential number such as INV-0001. The Next number button adds one for you. Never reuse a number, and issue a credit note instead of deleting an invoice.

Can I add tax or VAT?

Yes. Enter a rate and a name for the tax, and untick the Tax box on any line that is exempt. You can also say that your prices already include tax.

Can I give a discount?

Yes, as a percentage or a fixed amount. The discount is applied before tax, and the tax is reduced in proportion.

What are payment terms?

They say when payment is due, such as Net 30 for 30 days after the invoice date. Choosing a term sets the due date for you.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Upload an image and it appears at the top. It is resized in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.

Can I make a quote or credit note?

Yes. Change the document type to quote, proforma invoice or credit note, and the heading and wording change with it.

Can I mark an invoice as paid?

Use the status stamp, or enter an amount already paid so that the balance due is calculated for you.

Can I save my details for next time?

Tick Remember my business details. They are stored in this browser only. You can also save a full draft as a file and load it later.

Can I send it by email?

Download it as a PDF or HTML file and attach it to an email. The tool does not send email for you.

Which currencies are supported?

Ten common currency symbols, including dollar, euro, pound, rupee and dirham. The symbol is shown in front of every amount.

Does it support several tax rates on one invoice?

It applies one rate to the lines marked as taxable. For several rates, create separate invoices or add the tax as separate lines.

Does this meet e-invoicing rules?

No. Some countries require structured electronic invoices. This tool produces a visual document, so check your local rules.

Is my data stored or sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Data is saved only if you tick the remember box, and then only on your device.
