Contact

We would like to hear from you. Email us at contact@utiliby.com.

What to write to us about

  • A bug or a wrong result in one of our tools. Please include the tool name and the values you entered.
  • A correction or a suggestion to make a page clearer.
  • A tool you would like us to build.
  • Business, partnership or press enquiries.

We read every message, but we cannot always reply quickly. Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information by email.
